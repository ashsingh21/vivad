# NCR research notes

Facts gathered from Wikipedia on 2026-10-31 to ground the 100-player, 2 km-per-city version of the map. Each line names the article it came from. Numbers are as the article states them.

## The four cities

- **Gurugram** (Gurgaon): about 30 km southwest of New Delhi, in Haryana. Population 876,969 (2011). Known for air pollution and flooding from limited drainage. *Gurgaon*
- **DLF Cyber City** (est. 2003): IT and Fortune 500 offices; Cyber Hub is its shopping and dining street. NH-48 runs through it, with Udyog Vihar (older industry) across the highway. Rapid Metro loops inside Cyber City; Gateway Tower marks its entrance. *DLF Cyber City*
- **Noida**: planned city founded 1976 in the floodplain between the Yamuna and the Hindon. Over 50% green cover. Population 642,381 (2011). *Noida*
- **Noida Film City**: Sector 16-A, about 100 acres (75 outdoor, 25 indoor), 16 studios and news channel buildings. *Noida Film City*
- **Okhla Bird Sanctuary**: about 4 km² at the Okhla barrage, a lake made by damming the Yamuna; thorny scrub, grassland and wetland. *Okhla Sanctuary*
- **Ghaziabad**: Uttar Pradesh, population 1,729,000, a major North India rail junction. Split by the Hindon into Trans-Hindon (west, towards Delhi) and Cis-Hindon (east). *Ghaziabad*
- **Indirapuram**: residential sub-city of Ghaziabad founded 1996, about 5 km from the Vaishali and Noida Electronic City metro stations. *Indirapuram*
- **Sahibabad**: industrial, residential and commercial areas of Ghaziabad touching the Delhi and Noida borders. *Sahibabad*
- The **Hindon** joins the Yamuna near Gautam Buddha University in Noida. *Hindon River*

## Delhi landmarks

- **Connaught Place**: built 1929–1933, designed by Robert Tor Russell, Rajiv Chowk metro station beneath. *Connaught Place*
- **Kartavya Path**: ceremonial east–west boulevard from Rashtrapati Bhavan through Vijay Chowk and India Gate to the National Stadium, lined with lawns, canals and tree rows; Janpath crosses it. *Kartavya Path*
- **India Gate**: war memorial arch by Lutyens at the eastern end of the ceremonial axis. *India Gate*
- **Chandni Chowk**: Old Delhi market built 1650; the Red Fort sits at its eastern end. *Chandni Chowk*
- **Red Fort**: Mughal fort begun 1639 on the Yamuna bank; flag raised at the Lahori Gate. *Red Fort*
- **Delhi Ridge**: quartzite ridge of the Aravalli, about 35 km from Tughlaqabad to Wazirabad on the Yamuna; "green lungs" of the city. *Delhi Ridge*

## Crossings and transport

- **Signature Bridge**: cable-stayed, Wazirabad to East Delhi; the pylon is Delhi's tallest structure, with a 154 m viewing box. *Signature Bridge*
- **Old Yamuna Bridge** (Loha Pul): double-decked steel truss, 1866, 804.67 m long, 12 spans of 61.72 m. *Old Yamuna Bridge*
- **DND Flyway**: 8 lanes, 7.5 km, Maharani Bagh / Nizamuddin to Noida Sector 15A. *DND Flyway*
- **Blue Line**: mostly elevated; main line Dwarka Sector 21 to Noida Electronic City (56.11 km, 50 stations); branch Yamuna Bank to Vaishali (8.51 km, 8 stations). *Blue Line (Delhi Metro)*
- **Yellow Line**: Samaypur Badli to Millennium City Centre Gurugram (formerly HUDA City Centre), 49.02 km, mostly underground; Chawri Bazar station is about 25 m deep. *Yellow Line (Delhi Metro)*
- **Noida–Greater Noida Expressway**: six lanes, 24.53 km. *Noida–Greater Noida Expressway*

## Heights (for the skyline)

- Cyberthum towers, Noida: about 214 m, 50 floors. Amaryllis Iconic Tower, Delhi: 208 m. Trump Towers, Gurugram: 201.5 m, 55 floors. *List of tallest buildings in India*
- Supernova Spira, Noida: designed for 300 m, construction on hold above floor 64. *Supernova Spira*
- So the NCR skyline tops out around 200–215 m today, with Signature Bridge's pylon as Delhi's tallest structure.

## What this means for the map

1. **Ten-to-one compression no longer fits.** With each city about 2 km across, the arena is roughly 4.5 × 4.5 km including the Yamuna band: about 20 km², 5 players per km² at 100 players (PUBG's 4 × 4 km Sanhok is about 6).
2. **Real order stays.** Delhi and Gurugram west of the Yamuna, Noida and Ghaziabad east; the Hindon between Noida and Ghaziabad (Trans-Hindon Ghaziabad faces Delhi); Okhla at the Yamuna–Noida corner; the Ridge in Delhi's west.
3. **Heights to use.** Gurugram and Noida towers up to about 200 m, Delhi mostly low with a 154 m bridge pylon, Ghaziabad societies mid-rise.
4. **Signature crossings.** Signature Bridge (north, tall pylon), Loha Pul (two decks), Blue Line viaduct, DND Flyway (8 lanes, open). Real spans are 800 m+; in game they become 150–250 m.
5. **Transport is now needed.** At 4.5 km, crossing on foot takes over 10 minutes; vehicles (autos, bikes, cars) and the metro become part of the game, not decoration.
