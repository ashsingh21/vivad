# NCR visual targets

These images communicate the intended look of the proposed 4 × 4 km map. They are AI-generated concept art, not captures of the playable build. The canonical plan remains `../ncr-4km.plan.json`; imagery is not a dimensional or performance validation.

## Full-map aerial concept

![Four-city aerial concept](ncr-aerial-concept-v1.png)

Delhi is northwest, Gurugram southwest, Ghaziabad northeast and Noida southeast, separated by the Yamuna corridor. The tactical atlas supplied the layout reference. Building detail, terrain and infrastructure in this image are illustrative.

## Delhi / Ring Bazaar

![Delhi street-level concept](delhi-ring-bazaar-concept-v1.png)

The street-level view establishes material detail, arcade proportions, contemporary clothing, a divine bow and a readable metro entrance. Background crowds, specific vehicles and the distant skyline are illustrative art details, not additional implemented features.
