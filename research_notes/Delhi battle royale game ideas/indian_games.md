# Indian and India-set games: local flavour and how Indian players reacted

Checked up to 1 November 2026. Tags: **[PLAYER OPINION]** is what players said. **[CRITIC]** is the view of a press reviewer or essayist. **[DEV CLAIM]** is a figure or statement from a developer or publisher, including playtest numbers they released. Untagged lines are press-reported facts. Reddit could not be reached, so player evidence comes only from TapTap, Metacritic and NeoGAF. Treat it as a small sample, not as Indian players overall.

## 1. What did Indus, FAU-G, BGMI, Free Fire, Mumbai Gullies, Raji and others do with Indian culture?

### Takeaway
In the sources read, most of these games put Indian culture on the surface: names, skins, emotes, festival sales and celebrity faces. Very few changed how a match plays. Two 2026 events show the limits: Indus moved to a Brazilian favela setting on the same engine, and Mumbai Gullies stopped development without ever releasing a public build.

### Inferences
- Prime Rush suggests Indus's Indian identity was a replaceable layer, since the same engine could take a favela setting. Panga should put Delhi into the rules of play (the Aandhi, chai healing, street routes) so it cannot be swapped out.
- A small map is the right answer to the Mumbai Gullies story. The trailer showed that recognisable streets and vehicles excite people, while the failure came from scope. Panga's 500 m Connaught Place can deliver that recognition at a size a tiny team can finish.
- Chappal and tractor jokes both draw on everyday Indian life rather than myth, and they are cheap to make. These are the closest examples to the local feel the owner wants.
- Free Fire India's reported focus is on compliance and Indian servers, not visible Delhi or Indian content. Going by that one article, street-level Indian detail remains open ground.

- I found no India-specific evidence on snipers or hiding places. No source I read discusses them.

## 2. What worked, what looked fake or cringe, and what did players complain about?

### Takeaway
The Indian setting earns attention but does not save a weak game. Players and critics mainly complained about gun feel, performance, copying and pay-to-win. Thin culture, such as characters with no story and an empty map, was a secondary complaint. Patriotic framing drew a cash-in charge from at least one major reviewer.

### Cited Findings

**What worked**
- **[DEV CLAIM]** Nearly 90% of FAU-G: Domination playtesters called the Indian environments and themes a standout feature, and about 70% rated gunplay good to excellent. No sample size is given, and the page date of March 2026 does not fit its pre-launch content — [The Tech Panda](https://thetechpanda.com/fau-g-dominations-recent-playtests-reveal-what-india-wants-from-made-in-india-fps-games/41221/); [MediaNews4U](https://www.medianews4u.com/nearly-90-of-players-believe-that-the-indian-environments-and-themes-in-game-are-a-standout-feature-in-fau-g-domination/)
- The Mumbai Gullies trailer excited many Indian gamers, who hailed it as India's first AAA game. The community's YouTube following grew from 20K to over 117K — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-originals/mumbai-gullies-the-rise-and-fall-of-indias-most-hyped-game)
- **[CRITIC]** Indian-themed characters, weapons and emotes gave Indus a distinct feel, and the review called it a heartfelt first step needing more polish — [India Today Gaming](https://www.indiatodaygaming.com/reviews/story/indus-battle-royale-review-an-ambitious-desi-spin-on-the-genre-but-can-it-keep-up-5951)
- Indian gamers at home and abroad cheered Raji's release — [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/raji-ancient-indian-epic-far-right-hindu-nationalism/). **[PLAYER OPINION]** One Indian Metacritic user enjoyed the care given to mythology — [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/raji-an-ancient-epic/)

**What looked thin or fake**
- **[CRITIC]** Indus's cultural nods were mostly cosmetic. Characters looked good but had no distinct abilities or backstories, and Virlok was dull — [India Today Gaming](https://www.indiatodaygaming.com/reviews/story/indus-battle-royale-review-an-ambitious-desi-spin-on-the-genre-but-can-it-keep-up-5951)
- **[CRITIC]** A review by a former SuperGaming employee, who disclosed this, said much of Indus did not match the Indo-futuristic vision sold before release. Examples were generic names such as Nova and Triton, cookie-cutter avatars and weapon skins, a ninja skin resembling Fortnite's Peely, and an announcer voice that sounded sloppy and possibly AI-generated. It did praise Gufa's architecture — [High Chaos Run](https://highchaosrun.com/2024/10/31/indus-battle-royale-review/)
- **[PLAYER OPINION]** One October 2024 TapTap post, with several replies agreeing, called Indus's map empty and its characters boring. It said the game copied Apex Legends and Valorant, its guns looked like Apex and Call of Duty, and it felt incomplete — [TapTap post](https://www.taptap.io/post/8262360)
- **[PLAYER OPINION]** One TapTap reviewer accused the developers of relying on Indian fan loyalty instead of quality — [TapTap reviews](https://www.taptap.io/app/278431/review)
- **[CRITIC]** India Today called FAU-G a rushed attempt to profit from nationalist feeling around Republic Day — [Wikipedia, citing India Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fearless_and_United_Guards)
- **[CRITIC]** TechRaptor said Raji sometimes felt like edutainment — [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/reviews/raji-ancient-epic). Smashpad said it was not much fun to play — [Smashpad](https://smashpad.com/raji-an-ancient-epic-review/)

**What players and critics complained about**
- **[CRITIC]** Indus had clunky shooting, poor hit registration, frame drops on high-end and budget phones, no prone or lean, and only one vehicle — [India Today Gaming](https://www.indiatodaygaming.com/reviews/story/indus-battle-royale-review-an-ambitious-desi-spin-on-the-genre-but-can-it-keep-up-5951)
- **[PLAYER OPINION]** Players reported odd recoil, slow movement, blurry graphics and pay-to-win equipment in Indus — [TapTap post](https://www.taptap.io/post/8262360)
- Players review-bombed the 2021 FAU-G, comparing it badly with PUBG, and it fell from top free game to 11th. Its founder called the one-star ratings an anomaly — [Wikipedia, citing Gadgets 360](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fearless_and_United_Guards). Critic scores included 2/5 from Indian Express and TechRadar and 4.5/10 from India Today — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fearless_and_United_Guards)
- **[PLAYER OPINION]** FAU-G: Domination has 6.3/10 from only 64 TapTap ratings, with some players calling it fun and others a Call of Duty copy with heavy monetisation — [TapTap](https://www.taptap.io/app/33746814). This contrasts with the 4.4 stars cited on Google Play — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/fau-g-domination-sees-surge-in-engagement-in-india)
- Views on Indus monetisation conflict: **[CRITIC]** India Today called it fair — [India Today Gaming](https://www.indiatodaygaming.com/reviews/story/indus-battle-royale-review-an-ambitious-desi-spin-on-the-genre-but-can-it-keep-up-5951), while **[CRITIC]** the former employee's review objected to repeated in-game battle pass prompts — [High Chaos Run](https://highchaosrun.com/2024/10/31/indus-battle-royale-review/)
- **[DEV CLAIM]** Roby John said Indus was built with Apex Legends-level complexity, but the audience it drew was mostly Free Fire players — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/esports/esports-originals/what-happened-to-supergamings-inr-25-crore-tournament)
- As of April 2026, the INR 2 crore Indus final was still on hold and that prize had not been paid. Qualified players had been waiting since early 2025 — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/esports/esports-originals/what-happened-to-supergamings-inr-25-crore-tournament)
- In October 2024 SuperGaming filed a police complaint (FIR) against a YouTuber over content it called abusive, then withdrew it after he acknowledged the harm caused to the team — [GamingOnPhone](https://gamingonphone.com/news/indus-battle-royale-creators-supergaming-files-fir-against-youtuber-over-abusive-content)
- A forum post says the same dispute included copyright strikes on the creator's videos and drew criticism from other creators. This source is weak — [iQOO Community](https://community.iqoo.com/in/thread/67795). SuperGaming had earlier said it would not strike creators unless they misrepresented the game or broke its creator guidelines — [AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/games/all-you-need-to-know-about-supergamings-indus-made-in-india-battle-royale-game-for-pc-mobile-and-consoles/)
- **[PLAYER OPINION]** Some forum users doubted early that GameEon had the resources for Mumbai Gullies — [NeoGAF](https://www.neogaf.com/threads/mumbai-gullies-gta-inspired-game-set-in-mumbai.1643215/)
- Mumbai Gullies founder Nikhil Malankar's own lesson was: "scope smaller, ship faster, protect your runway." — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-originals/mumbai-gullies-the-rise-and-fall-of-indias-most-hyped-game)

### Inferences
- Indian players seem to judge a desi shooter against PUBG, Apex and Free Fire, with no discount for being Indian. Panga's gun feel and smooth running must come before more content.
- Local names on copied systems read as fake. A character needs a reason to exist in Delhi, not only an Indian name on a generic hero.
- Picking the audience early matters. Indus's mismatch suggests Panga should decide whether it serves quick casual players or skilled players. Its 7 to 9 minute match leans casual.
- Nationalism gives a launch spike but invites a cash-in charge. Panga's street humour is a safer tone than war or patriotism.
- Do not promise prizes or big events a tiny team cannot be sure of delivering.
- A public fight with a creator can become the bigger story. Panga should set clear creator rules early and allow clips and reviews, including harsh ones.

### Gaps
- No Reddit, Google Play or App Store review text was read, so "cringe" is not measured directly. The only reachable player comments were on TapTap, Metacritic and NeoGAF, which may not reflect typical Indian players.
- The FIR details come from search summaries, not full reads of the GamingOnPhone and iQOO pages. Whether the copyright strikes were later removed is unknown. High Chaos Run links an IGN article on this dispute, which I did not find.
- I found no critic review of FAU-G: Domination.

## 3. What do Indian players want from a desi game, with evidence?

### Takeaway
Evidence here is the weakest in these notes. The best-supported wants are a game that runs well on their device, core shooting as good as the big imports, no pay-to-win, and real Indian places. Strong demand for Hindi or humour is plausible but barely measured.

### Cited Findings
- **Indian settings:** **[DEV CLAIM]** Nearly 90% named Indian environments and themes as a standout feature — [The Tech Panda](https://thetechpanda.com/fau-g-dominations-recent-playtests-reveal-what-india-wants-from-made-in-india-fps-games/41221/). The same article names no specific place or theme.
- **Simple, fast modes:** **[DEV CLAIM]** 55% preferred Team Deathmatch over Arms Race, Deathmatch and Search and Destroy — [The Tech Panda](https://thetechpanda.com/fau-g-dominations-recent-playtests-reveal-what-india-wants-from-made-in-india-fps-games/41221/)
- **Casual players:** FAU-G: Domination reportedly resonated with casual gamers, and the article names Delhi, Pune, Patna and Lucknow as markets — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/fau-g-domination-sees-surge-in-engagement-in-india). Indus's audience was mostly Free Fire players — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/esports/esports-originals/what-happened-to-supergamings-inr-25-crore-tournament)
- **Device performance:** **[DEV CLAIM]** 24% of playtesters felt their devices overheating — [The Tech Panda](https://thetechpanda.com/fau-g-dominations-recent-playtests-reveal-what-india-wants-from-made-in-india-fps-games/41221/). **[CRITIC]** India Today questioned Indus's lack of low-end support — [India Today Gaming](https://www.indiatodaygaming.com/reviews/story/indus-battle-royale-review-an-ambitious-desi-spin-on-the-genre-but-can-it-keep-up-5951)
- **Quality with no discount:** Players punished FAU-G for falling short of PUBG — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fearless_and_United_Guards). **[PLAYER OPINION]** One TapTap user could not understand why anyone still supported Indus, and rejected the argument that it was only a beta — [TapTap post](https://www.taptap.io/post/8262360)
- **Fair play:** **[PLAYER OPINION]** The same post objected to top equipment being purchasable — [TapTap post](https://www.taptap.io/post/8262360)
- **Hype and real demand can differ:** Mumbai Gullies reached over 117K YouTube followers but only about 16K Steam wishlists — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-originals/mumbai-gullies-the-rise-and-fall-of-indias-most-hyped-game)
- **Attention stays with the big games:** Most streams of completed Indus phases drew under 1K total views, while BGMI Series 2024 peaked above 274K concurrent viewers — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/esports/esports-originals/what-happened-to-supergamings-inr-25-crore-tournament)
- **Goofing around with local vehicles:** Indian Bike Driving 3D players seek out codes for a tractor and other heavy vehicles — [Cashify](https://www.cashify.in/indian-bike-driving-3d-cheat-codes); [TikTok tag](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/indian-bike-driving-3d-tractor-cheat-code)

### Inferences
- A rough order of wants, with low confidence: runs smoothly, good gun feel, fair money system, real Indian places, then Hindi and humour.
- The tractor is backed by player behaviour rather than a poll. Unlocking one is enough to fuel videos, so a tractor in Panga could be a shareable moment even if it is slow and silly.
- Followers and wishlists should be read separately. Panga should treat views as interest and measure actual play.
- Desktop first may miss India's largest audience, since the evidence above centres on phones. This fits the plan for phones later, but it means phone-ready performance should stay in mind.

### Gaps
- No independent survey of Indian player wants was found. The only numbers come from a developer playtest with no stated sample size, and it names no liked places or themes.
- The playtest date is unclear. The Tech Panda page shows March 2026, but the text is pre-launch and compares against July 2024. An [IndianTelevision URL](https://indiantelevision.com/iworld/gaming/fau-g:-domination-playtests-uncover-india%E2%80%99s-fps-game-demands-241025) suggests October 2024.
- No download or player counts were found for Indian Bike Driving 3D, so its true size is unknown.
- Gaming-creator comments and Hindi-language forums were not searched.

## 4. Which Indian voice, humour, music, festival or cricket features landed well?

### Takeaway
Festival events and cricket tie-ins are clearly the industry's standard approach, but none of the coverage measures player reaction, so "landed well" cannot be proven here. The nearest thing to praise is for natural-sounding mixed English and Hindi lines. Celebrity deals look out of reach for a tiny team, and FAU-G shows a star does not rescue a weak game.

### Cited Findings

**Voice**
- **[CRITIC]** Sportskeeda's profile said Harbor's lines felt natural and not stale — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/valorant/meet-sunil-malhotra-voice-actor-valorant-s-new-indian-controller-agent-harbor). A different Sportskeeda piece credits another actor, so the credit conflicts — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/valorant/valorant-a-glimpse-voice-actors-behind-agents-game)
- An esports team owner called Harbor highly significant for Valorant in India. This is an industry view, not a player one — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/valorant/connection-indian-audience-going-lot-deeper-enigma-gaming-s-owner-shares-significance-harbor-s-release-valorant)
- Liquipedia credits Hrithik Roshan as performing Jai, but does not confirm Hindi audio — [Liquipedia](https://liquipedia.net/freefire/Jai)

## 5. Which cultural or religious content caused controversy in Indian games or media?

### Takeaway
The biggest backlash came from sacred places or figures being shown in a violent or disrespectful way. Slang put in the mouth of a revered character can also cause serious trouble, as Adipurush shows. Objections come from different directions: Sikh and Hindu groups, film audiences, and critics writing about caste and minorities.

### Cited Findings
- **Hitman 2 (2002):** Sikh youth complained about a temple level where players were told to shoot men in turbans as terrorists. The setting resembled the Golden Temple, the site of Operation Blue Star in 1984. Eidos apologised and agreed to remove scenes and change later editions — [Hinduism Today](https://www.hinduismtoday.com/hpi/2002/11/21/2002-11-21-sikh-kids-force-changes-in-video-game/)
- A fan wiki says patch 1.02 altered all India-set levels and removed Indian god graphics and Sikh references — [Hitman Wiki](https://hitman.fandom.com/wiki/Temple_City_Ambush)
- **[PLAYER OPINION]** Not all Sikhs agreed: a Sikh Times piece carries one player's view that the reaction was excessive — [The Sikh Times](http://www.sikhtimes.com/news_122402a.html)
- **Smite (early 2010s):** After complaints about its depiction of Kali, Hi-Rez removed the image from its website. Rajan Zed of the Universal Society of Hinduism called this a step forward but wanted her out of the game — [The Escapist forums](https://forums.escapistmagazine.com/threads/smite-dev-removes-offensive-goddess-from-website.108200/)
- **Adipurush (film, 2023):** Hanuman's street-style lines were mocked as tapori and chapri — [Onmanorama](https://www.onmanorama.com/entertainment/entertainment-news/2023/06/18/adipurush-movie-hanuman-dialogue-social-media-backlash.html)
- The writer first defended the lines as meant to connect with a younger generation, then the makers changed them — [DailyO](https://www.dailyo.in/entertainment/adipurush-dialogues-being-changed-says-writer-manoj-muntashir-day-after-defending-his-tapori-words-40164); [Onmanorama](https://www.onmanorama.com/entertainment/entertainment-news/2023/06/22/adipurush-movie-controversy-dialogues-hanuman-revised.html)
- In new prints, the word baap (father) in the line was replaced with Lanka — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/entertainment/adipurush-dialogues-changed-jalegi-tere-baap-ki-amended-version-goes-viral)
- The writer cited a threat to his life and Mumbai police gave him security — [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/nation/adipurush-row-dialogue-writer-manoj-shukla-cites-threat-to-life-mumbai-cops-provide-security-518509/amp)
- **Raji (2020):** **[CRITIC]** Vice argued it told Durga's story only from the winners' side, ignored Adivasi communities who mourn Mahishasura, used casteist language about polluting demons, and left Muslims out of an Indo-Islamic-looking world — [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/raji-ancient-indian-epic-far-right-hindu-nationalism/)
- **The word chapri:** It is traced to the Chapparband caste, a community once listed under the colonial Criminal Tribes Act, and is now used as a class insult. Sources are mostly essays, and they differ on how harmful it is today. Rapper MC Stan has tried to reclaim it — [Medium essay](https://medium.com/@akshayshayana1771/chapri-a-castiest-and-classist-slur-that-polices-the-urban-poor-00d49da7ac8f); [FLAME University](https://www.flame.edu.in/in-the-media/digital-exclusion-how-chapri-and-digital-aesthetics-reinforce-caste-hierarchies)
- **Real conflict:** SuperGaming postponed the Indus final in May 2025, citing India-Pakistan tensions after Operation Sindoor — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/esports/esports-originals/what-happened-to-supergamings-inr-25-crore-tournament)

### Inferences
- Do not make real temples, gurdwaras, mosques or dargahs into fight arenas or loot spots. Hitman 2 shows this can force changes to a shipped game.
- Do not make gods or saints into playable characters, skins, weapons or comic figures.
- Never use a turban, skullcap or other religious marker as a sign of being an enemy or villain.
- Keep slang on ordinary street characters. Never put it in the mouth of a revered figure. Adipurush shows that a line meant to sound modern can turn into a safety problem for the writer.
- Keep chapri out of scripted lines, along with caste and community insults generally. Because voice and text are hard to change after launch, ask Delhi readers from varied backgrounds to review the script first.
- Stay away from real wars and border clashes. Panga's made-up Aandhi storm is a safer enemy than any real one.
- Rival mohalla or neighbourhood jokes are safer than religious or caste ones, though this still needs a local check.

- I did not research whether Panga's Trishul Carbine and Vajra-9 could draw objections. Both names come from sacred weapons in Hindu tradition (general knowledge, not sourced here). I found no case of backlash to such a name, so this needs a separate check.
