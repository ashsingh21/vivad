# Battle Royale Design: What Makes Players Return, and What Panga Can Copy

Evidence labels: **[Official]** is a developer or publisher's own statement or patch note. **[Data]** is a measured number, often from a third party. **[Study]** is academic work. **[Press]** is reporting or a fan guide. **[Opinion]** is a judgement by a writer, player or reviewer. Executive statements about their own game are marked **[Official]** but are self-reported. Panga facts come from the vivad repo as read on 2026-11-01 and are marked **[Repo]**. `bots.rs` was being edited while this was written, so repo references use symbol names rather than line numbers. All design advice sits under Inferences and is my judgement, not evidence.

## 1. What do PUBG, BGMI, Free Fire, Fortnite, Apex Legends and Warzone each do that is most loved?

### Takeaway
Each hit owns one simple promise: Free Fire fits a short gap on a cheap phone, Apex makes teamwork work without a mic, Warzone turns your first death into a fight, Fortnite's short formats drew huge numbers, and BGMI sells itself as Indian culture. No source measures "most loved" directly, so for PUBG and BGMI this rests on what the developers protect and promote, not on player data.

### Cited Findings

**Free Fire**
- [Official] Garena's store listing sells the game on 10 minutes, 50 players and fast, light play, and lists Clash Squad as a fast 4v4 mode — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/pk/app/id1300146617?l=en-gb)
- [Official] The same listing names hiding as a feature: hide in the wild or go unseen by lying prone in grass or rifts. It also promotes driving vehicles across the map — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/pk/app/id1300146617?l=en-gb)
- [Official] Producer Harold Teo defines accessibility as fitting how people use phones in short gaps. He calls a light install a team priority, because a 4 GB to 8 GB game makes people think twice. The article puts the 2017 release at about 200 MB — [FreeFireMania](https://www.freefiremania.com.br/news/free-fire-producer-talks-about-the-future-explains-pubg-process-and-more.html)
- [Official] Conflict on length: the store says 10 minutes, but Teo says a match lasts at most 20 minutes, with shorter modes at 5 to 10 minutes (the article calls Clash Squad "Contra Squad") — [FreeFireMania](https://www.freefiremania.com.br/news/free-fire-producer-talks-about-the-future-explains-pubg-process-and-more.html). The listing is now 1.5 GB — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/pk/app/id1300146617?l=en-gb)

**Apex Legends**
- [Official] Design director Mackey McCandlish said playtesting showed three-person squads were a space Respawn could own. He said a 60-player cap gives a squad a 5% win chance, so the hope of winning pays off more often — [Press Start](https://press-start.com.au/news/playstation/2019/02/05/heres-why-apex-legends-opted-for-a-different-player-count-to-other-battle-royales/). **Warzone**
- [Opinion] The Gulag sends a player to a 1v1 duel after a first death, and winning brings them back. Before launch, Mein-MMO argued its value is removing the idle dead time of squad battle royales. GamesRadar called it the best way to play on Verdansk (headline only) — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/resurgence-on-verdansk-is-now-the-best-way-to-play-call-of-duty-warzone)

**BGMI**

**PUBG**

Normal reportedly stays at 28m 50s, a figure seen in a search summary of the 42.1 notes rather than on a page I opened — [Mp1st](https://mp1st.com/title-updates-and-patches/pubg-update-1-017-drops-to-bring-42-1-on-consoles). The developers say the rework's aim is "not simply to increase pressure or force faster engagements" — [PUBG Dev Letter](https://www.pubg.com/en/news/10280)

### Inferences
- The hits can each be described in one line. Panga's could be 8 minutes, 16 players, real Connaught Place.
- BGMI's local feel lives on skins and locations rather than in new mechanics. That is the cheapest kind of flavour for a tiny team, but BGMI's tie-ups are licensed. Panga needs invented brands, as it already does with its made-up shops.
- **[Repo]** The death lines already carry the slang the owner wants ("Tapak gaye", "PANGA KING!", "R dabao, phir se panga lo" in `crates/game/src/hud.rs`). The risk now is the mythic leftovers: `assets/data/items.ron` still has Brahmastra and Sudarshan Chakra, and `crates/shared/src/sim/flow.rs` still has the Pushpaka Vimana. If players see these names, they pull against a Delhi street tone.
- A solo player's odds in Panga are 1 in 16 (6.25%) with equal skill, better than the 5% Apex designed around. Keeping bots beatable matters more than adding win-rate tricks.
- Hiding is a headline feature in the biggest mobile rival, which supports the owner's ask.
- Pings are not worth building until Panga has online squads.

### Gaps
- I found no cross-game player survey of the most loved features. Quantic Foundry has no dedicated battle royale study that I could find.
- PUBG's most loved features are unmeasured here. The notes above cover what developers protect, not what players say.
- Warzone's Cash, Buy Stations, Contracts and Loadout Drops have no source I could open. PC Gamer and GamesRadar pages loaded as headlines only, and Wikipedia returned a disambiguation page.
- One search summary said Respawn banned voice chat in internal ping testing. The likely source blocked access, so treat this as unverified.
- Teo's interview is a third-party write-up, likely translated, so his wording may have shifted.

## 2. Which mechanics matter most in short matches on small maps with few players?

### Takeaway
Short formats work by cutting the slow parts, not by speeding everything up: fewer players, a start that guarantees a quick fight, loot that needs no sorting, and a fast way back in. Panga is about twice as crowded as the densest format I could size, so its main risk is early death rather than boredom. Both of PUBG's tiny maps also added systems that find or force out hiders.

### Inferences
- **Density.** Total map area per player is about 62,500 m² on Karakin (a 250 m square), 31,250 m² on Haven (177 m) and 15,625 m² on Panga (125 m). Buildings and blocked ground make Panga tighter still. A mid-game lull is unlikely, while a lethal first minute is likely.
- **Landing spread may matter most.** Straight Shot puts two teams at each POI to control early fights. **[Repo]** Each Panga bot leaves the flight at a random point between 15% and 85% of the path, if it is inside the map bounds (`drop_at` in `crates/shared/src/sim/bots.rs`), so landings follow one line. Check whether this causes early pile-ups.
- **Panga has already made the Straight Shot loot cut.** `assets/data/items.ron` uses rarity as a damage scale and defines no attachments. Keep it.
- **Hiding spots need a counter.** Hiding is a selling point in Free Fire, yet PUBG added an anti-hiding system to both of its tiny maps. If Panga adds hiding places, it should add a way to flush people out in the same update.
- **Vehicles are not transport on this map.** Sprinting crosses 500 m in about 68 s. The worst-case run to the first safe circle is about 300 m, or 40 s, against 110 s available. Nobody needs a car to beat the ring, so a tractor or auto must earn its place as moving cover, a ram or a funny moment.
- **Snipers have a short window.** Once the ring is 120 m wide at 285 s, long range stops mattering. **[Repo]** The Gulel already acts as the sniper: 95 damage, 1.2 s between shots, the fastest projectile, zero spread. A scoped gun should be tuned for roughly the first five minutes and may overlap with the Gulel.

### Gaps
- Free Fire map sizes and zone timings have no reliable source here. Results were low-quality fan pages.
- Match lengths for Karakin, Haven and Reload were not found.
- Whether Blitz has respawns is unconfirmed: one guide says no, and Automaton is silent.
- One guide said Rebirth Island caps at 40 players, but I could not tie that to a page I opened, so it is not in the table.
- I found no sourced data on sniper balance such as crate-only rifles. Any other researcher covering weapons should take this.
- No source compares retention between short and long modes. Popularity is evidence of interest, not of long-term return.

## 3. How do they handle the drop, early loot, mid match lull, zone pressure and the final fight?

### Takeaway
PUBG's June 2026 Blue Zone letter is the most useful primary source: it names two opposite failures, matches that stall after looting and endings where movement, combat and zone damage all pile up at once. Its fixes are cheap to copy, especially damage based on time spent outside the zone rather than distance from it.

### Cited Findings

**The drop**
- [Official] Straight Shot removes the Jumpmaster. Players spawn above a random POI and drop straight in — [EA](https://www.ea.com/da/games/apex-legends/apex-legends/news/fifth-anniversary-collection-event)
- **[Repo]** Panga's flight is 120 m high at 22 m/s (`crates/shared/src/sim/flow.rs`), and the ring comment assumes a half-minute ride.

**Early loot**
- [Official] Pre-kitted weapons at set rarities replace attachment hunting in Straight Shot — [EA](https://www.ea.com/da/games/apex-legends/apex-legends/news/fifth-anniversary-collection-event)
- [Press] In Blitz every player starts with random weapons and abilities — [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/fortnite-20250710-348661/). In Darwin Project everyone starts with an axe, a bow and arrows — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darwin_Project)

**Mid-match lull**
- [Official] PUBG says Ranked teams settled inside the zone early, so matches stalled after looting and first rotations, and it retuned phases with too much downtime — [PUBG Dev Letter](https://www.pubg.com/en/news/10280)
- [Press] In Blitz, eliminations or surviving storm phases raise a level, and each level gives another random weapon and ability, so even quiet stretches pay out — [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/fortnite-20250710-348661/)
- [Official] PUBG uses map events to break stillness: Karakin's Black Zone and Haven's helicopter (see section 2).

**Zone pressure**
- [Official] The old ruleset dated from April 2023. In Normal matches, circles got smaller while shrink times got shorter, so movement, combat and damage pressure converged around Phases 5 and 6, causing late rotations, rushed entries, late vehicle reliance and clustering — [PUBG Dev Letter](https://www.pubg.com/en/news/10280)
- [Official] Damage now scales with time spent in the Blue Zone rather than distance from safety. Early slow rotations stay viable, but long exposure gets costly and healing in the zone while setting up a flank becomes much harder — [PUBG Dev Letter](https://www.pubg.com/en/news/10280)
- [Official] Normal warning times were cut slightly and shrink times extended, to give late fights more room. Ranked weakens zone-centering so circles are harder to predict, keeping some centering so late circles stay fair — [PUBG Dev Letter](https://www.pubg.com/en/news/10280)
- [Official] Timing changes begin at Phase 2. Player-count-based zones were removed from Sanhok and Karakin, with no reason given — [patched.gg](https://patched.gg/games/pubg-battlegrounds/patch-notes-update-421). The idea had been used on Savage, with timing tied to players left — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/au/pubgs-savage-map-has-a-blue-zone-that-adjusts-its-timing-based-on-the-number-of-players-left)
- [Official] PUBG will monitor survivors per phase, player density and engagement distance in safe zones, movement and combat pace, and whether zone damage runs above intent — [PUBG Dev Letter](https://www.pubg.com/en/news/10280)
- [Official] Hyper Scape used vanishing map sectors instead of a circle — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyper_Scape)

**The final fight**
- [Official] PUBG names two ways to get the ending wrong: Normal piled pressure up too fast, while Ranked kept late zones large and predictable, which reduced tension — [PUBG Dev Letter](https://www.pubg.com/en/news/10280)
- [Press] Reload stops reboots after the 7th zone closes, so the match turns into normal last-life play for the finish — [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/guides/fortnite-reload-explained/)
- [Official] Hyper Scape added a second win path: hold the crown for 60 seconds — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyper_Scape)

- **[Repo]** Ring damage ticks once per second through the normal damage path, so shields absorb it first (`crates/shared/src/sim/zone.rs`, `crates/shared/src/sim/combat.rs`). Warriors have 100 HP and up to 100 shield (`Health` in `crates/shared/src/sim/mod.rs`).
- **[Repo]** Haldi Doodh gives 25 HP in 2.5 s, up to a cap of 75 HP. Cutting Chai gives 25 shield in 2.0 s, and Lassi 50 shield in 4.5 s (`assets/data/items.ron`). Each lands all at once when the use finishes, averaging 10 to 12.5 points per second (`crates/shared/src/sim/combat.rs`).

### Inferences
- **The Aandhi can be out-healed.** At 1 to 7 damage per second in Phases 1 to 4, a player with stock can sit outside the ring and still come out ahead, mostly through shield items. In `combat.rs`, switching item cancels a heal but taking damage does not. This is the behaviour PUBG just moved to stop. It comes from reading the code and has not been tested in play.
- **Copy time-based damage first.** Track seconds outside per warrior and scale the tick with it. It needs one float, and it keeps a slow early rotation forgiving while ending ring-sitting. Bot logic that reasons about the Aandhi will need retuning.
- **Panga shares the pattern PUBG blames, but only partly.** Its shrink times also shorten each phase, from 45 s to 25 s. However, average closure falls from about 3.8 m/s to 0.4 m/s, under walking speed. Even the worst-case far edge in Phase 1, about 6.8 m/s once the circle's drift is added, stays under sprint speed. The movement pile-up should not apply, but combat and damage pressure still can.
- **The last ring could become a heal race.** Radius reaches 0, so nobody can be safe. At 14 per second, a full 200-point warrior lasts about 14 s plus healing, so a stalemate may go to whoever carries more chai rather than who shoots better. Measure how many matches end by ring rather than by weapon.
- **Be careful with shortening further.** PUBG's quoted warning applies directly: pressure alone is not the goal. Panga's 7.5 minute ring is already about a quarter of PUBG's Normal length.
- **Copy PUBG's monitoring list.** **[Repo]** `crates/shared/src/bin/sim_check.rs` already runs headless matches and reports each Aandhi death. Add survivors per phase, engagement distance, longest gap between kills, and share of deaths by ring. A tiny team gets PUBG-style telemetry from bots, with the caveat that bots do not behave like people.
- **Do not copy dynamic player-count zones yet.** PUBG removed them from two maps, though without saying why.

### Gaps
- The Dev Letter's per-phase timings and damage values are in images, so no numbers were available as text.
- The two sources differ in framing: patched.gg says the mid-game was sped up, while the letter says speed is not the goal.
- One search summary said early safe zones were reduced. Neither patched.gg nor the letter text states that, and PUBG's [Map Service Report](https://pubg.com/en/news/10181) was not opened.
- A search summary said Straight Shot's end ring closes in half the normal time. The likely article no longer exists, and Charlie Intel confirms only the 10-minute length, so ring speed is unverified.
- Apex ring tables, Fortnite storm tables and Warzone gas timings were not found. Warzone's mid-game systems (Contracts, public events) remain unsourced.
- Hot-drop survival rates and time-to-first-fight data were not found for any game.

## 4. What makes a player press play again?

### Takeaway
The evidence points to feeling capable and in control more than to winning itself, plus an almost instant restart and a way to soften early death. This is the weakest-evidenced question: two academic studies seen only at abstract level, plus developer claims. No study here tests what happens right after a loss.

### Cited Findings

Seen as a snippet only.

- **[Repo]** Panga's offline death screen already shows killer, placement and kills, then "R dabao, phir se panga lo" (`crates/game/src/hud.rs`). Online, players wait for the match to end.

### Inferences
- Panga already has the most-praised cheap feature, restart from the death screen. With bots there is no queue, so this holds at zero players. Protect that speed as content grows.
- Fortnite's reason for bots is the closest fit to Panga, whose whole lobby is bots. Consider easing bot skill after repeated early deaths and raising it after wins. **[Repo]** A `BotSkill` setting exists, read from the environment. Whether it can change between matches was not checked.
- The competence finding suggests giving players proof of improvement after a loss: placement against their last match, best placement, or kills compared with their record. This is an inference, since no study tests it directly.
- A small unlock track fits the interview finding without needing a live-service team, such as street titles or cosmetics earned by placement.
- Das's feedback loop is what a large team does. A tiny team cannot ship every two months at BGMI's scale, so variety from seeded rules is the cheaper substitute.

### Gaps
- I found no study on what happens in the seconds after a loss, on near-miss effects in battle royales, or on loss-chasing. Full texts of both studies were blocked (HTTP 403).
- A claimed Activision figure of a 90% retention drop and a claimed Helsinki 27% session-length rise both came from weak sources, could not be verified, and are excluded.
- No game here published D1 or D7 retention figures by mode, so the effect of bots, requeue and second chances on return rates is unmeasured.

## 5. What mistakes killed smaller battle royales such as Spellbreak, Hyper Scape and Darwin Project?

### Takeaway
All three ended with too few players rather than a proven design flaw, and good reviews did not save them: Spellbreak held 85% positive on Steam. None published a real postmortem, so anything beyond falling numbers is inference. Panga's bots remove the most direct cause, because the game still works with nobody else online.

### Cited Findings

**Hyper Scape (Ubisoft)**
- [Official] Revealed 2 July 2020, open beta 12 July, full release 11 August. Shutdown announced 27 January 2022 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyper_Scape). Servers closed 28 April 2022, and Ubisoft gave no reason beyond taking key learnings forward — [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/less-than-2-years-after-launch-ubisoft-is-closing-its-battle-royale-hyper-scape/amp/). Wikipedia also gives 27 April in one place.
- [Official] In October 2020 Ubisoft acknowledged it had missed player and company expectations and promised an overhaul — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyper_Scape)
- [Data] Metacritic scores were 68 on PC, 62 on PS4 and 60 on Xbox One, and OpenCritic showed 15% of critics recommending it. Critics praised graphics and map design, and faulted unbalanced gunplay and mechanics — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyper_Scape)
- [Data] It was briefly among the most-viewed Twitch games during beta, then saw very poor viewership a day after full release — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyper_Scape)
- [Press] PC Gamer's summary, seen only in a search snippet, says Ubisoft admitted within months that the game was too hard and somewhat boring — [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/hyper-scape-ubisofts-forgotten-battle-royale-is-shutting-down)

**Spellbreak (Proletariat)**
- [Data] About 5,500 peak Steam players at launch in late 2020, under 600 by mid-2021 — [SVG](https://svg.com/1164143/rip-spellbreak). TheGamer reported only 165 players over the prior 30 days at closure news, citing Steamcharts — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/spellbreak-servers-shut-down-developer-bought-by-blizzard/). The game was also crossplay on consoles and Switch, so Steam undercounts — [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/unique-battle-royale-with-wizards-is-85-positive-on-steam-still-need-to-close-now,844574)
- [Data] 85% positive Steam reviews, and last update on 1 September 2021 — [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/unique-battle-royale-with-wizards-is-85-positive-on-steam-still-need-to-close-now,844574)
- [Official] CEO Seth Sivak said it never reached a breakthrough or a sustainable position to justify more investment — [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/unique-battle-royale-with-wizards-is-85-positive-on-steam-still-need-to-close-now,844574)
- [Press] Blizzard's purchase of the studio, with about 100 staff moving to World of Warcraft, was reported on 29 June 2022, the same period as the closure news. TheGamer dates the decline to an April 2021 update that replaced team deathmatch with 5v5 zone control, which is correlation only — [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/spellbreak-servers-shut-down-developer-bought-by-blizzard/)
- [Official] Servers ended 10 January 2023, and a free Community Version allows offline and peer-to-peer play — [SVG](https://svg.com/1164143/rip-spellbreak)
- [Opinion] One writer says it did everything right on paper but failed to reach mainstream players in a crowded genre — [SVG](https://svg.com/1164143/rip-spellbreak)

**Darwin Project (Scavengers Studio)**
- [Official] The founders said the game could not sustain itself and that the playerbase was shrinking, after assessing all options. They had tried a PS4 port, classes, battle passes, new map tiles, live events and more spectator interaction — [TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2020/05/14/darwin-project-update-cancel-server-shutdown/)
- [Data] An 18-person team. Early access on 24 April 2018, full release 14 January 2020, development ended 13 May 2020. Metacritic 74 on PC, IGN 6/10 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darwin_Project)
- [Official] Each match needed 10 prisoners plus an eleventh human as Show Director — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darwin_Project)
- [Press] By January 2021 only two server regions remained, with low player counts cited — [MMOBomb](https://www.mmobomb.com/news/darwin-project-shuts-two-servers-today)

### Inferences
- **The shared killer is matchmaking dependence.** When concurrency falls, queues lengthen and more people leave. Panga's bots break that loop, a real structural advantage. It returns if online mode ever requires human lobbies, so keep bot fill.
- **A unique mechanic was not enough, and may have raised the entry cost.** Fast movement hacks, spell-glove combinations and a Show Director all had to be learned or staffed. Panga's hook is place and language, which costs players nothing to learn.
- **Darwin Project is the closest scale match and the clearest warning.** Small lobbies did not save it, and a star feature needing a live human performer cannot work without a crowd. An AI event director could keep the idea without the eleventh player.
- **Do not build for streamers first.** Hyper Scape's Twitch spike vanished within a day of full release.
- **Do not promise live-service content.** Darwin's 18-person team tried many additions and still could not hold players. Spellbreak players complained about too few updates. Avoid creating that expectation.
- **Spellbreak's Community Version shows the value of an offline mode.** It is the only form in which that game still runs. Panga has this by design.
- **Caveat:** Spellbreak's end was also tied to an acquisition, so it was a business outcome as much as a design one.

**Ranked copy list across all five questions.** Costs are my rough estimates, not sourced. The Delhi names are ideas only.

Keep, already built:
- Restart from the death screen, rarity-only guns, and slang death lines.

Small, days of work:
1. Time-based Aandhi damage (PUBG, 2026). Closes the out-heal gap.
2. Extra `sim_check` metrics: survivors per phase, engagement distance, longest gap between kills, and share of deaths by ring.
3. Spread the landing so no area takes too many warriors (Straight Shot's two-team POIs).
4. A building flush paired with any new hiding spots (Karakin's Black Zone without real destruction). Mark one building on the minimap, warn for about 20 s, then apply area damage inside. A Delhi skin could be a "sealing drive".

Medium, one to two weeks:
5. A second chance early in the match (Gulag, Reload). A plain 1v1 in a tiny room, Cricket Bat only, open until about Phase 2 or 3. It answers the owner's wish for fights, needs no gun balance, and could be called the Hawalat. Evidence favours keeping it 1v1 and simple, with no hiding spots. Reload's rule that reboots end at a fixed zone is the model for closing it.
6. Level-ups from kills or surviving ring phases (Blitz). Strong popularity evidence, but this needs balance work.
7. One seeded rule change per match (Karakin, Blitz) for variety without new content.

Later:
8. Pings, only when online squads exist.
9. Mobile download budget. **[Repo]** `assets/` is 496 MB today, 401 MB of it models, including untracked duplicates, so this is not a shipping size. Free Fire launched at about 200 MB.

Do not copy:
- Twitch integration, a cash economy, attachment systems, complex abilities, player-count-based zones, or a feature that needs a human director.
- Real brand tie-ups without a licence.

### Gaps
- Steam concurrency for Hyper Scape and Darwin Project was not found, and console numbers for all three are unknown.
- A search summary said Hyper Scape cut matches from 100 to 60 players and stopped updating in April 2021. Wikipedia states up to 100 and mentions no reduction, so this is unverified. Dot Esports and TweakTown were blocked.
- No revenue figures or team costs were found, so whether any of these could have survived at a smaller scale is unknown.
- No successful small battle royale from a tiny team was researched. That comparison would balance this list of failures and is the most useful follow-up.
