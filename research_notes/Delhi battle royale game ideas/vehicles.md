### Takeaway

### Inferences
- Warzone 1 trades about 15% of speed for 4 times the health (ATV to Bertha). Panga should tune by armour, exposure and noise, not by big speed gaps.
- The "fast but exposed, tough but slow and loud" pattern is shared by all five games, and it maps cleanly onto Delhi: bike, auto, tractor.
- Both auto rickshaw precedents are slow, quiet street vehicles. Panga can follow that and add a horn, which PUBG already proved as a small joy.
- Panga has no teams (no team, squad or duo code in `crates/shared/src/sim`), so extra seats have no job yet. Build a driver seat only, and add passengers if a team mode arrives.
- Solo drivers should give up their main gun, as in Apex. A Panga twist worth testing: a bike rider can swing the Cricket Bat (30 damage, 2.3 m reach, `assets/data/items.ron`).
- Fuel adds nothing on a 500 m map. A 3:30 Warzone-sized tank at 14 m/s is about 2,900 m, nearly 6 full crossings. Use health only at first.
- A sniper rifle pairs well with PUBG-style tyre damage or driver exposure. It gives the long gun a counter to vehicles without any new rule for rams.

### Gaps
- Fortnite speeds and exact fuel numbers: Fandom pages were blocked, so only health and rules came through search summaries. Sources also conflict on the launch patch: [13.40](https://fortnite-archive.fandom.com/wiki/13.40_Patch_Notes) against [13.30](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/fortnite-chapter-2-season-3-cars-update-v13-30-ps4-xbox-one-pc-switch-ios-android).
- Warzone 1 health and speed come from one site only, and Warzone seat-shooting rules were not confirmed because the official guide timed out.
- No official hearing distance for vehicles in any of the five games. One figure found (150 to 300 m) came from a Bohemia Interactive tracker, which is not PUBG, so it is excluded.
- Free Fire fuel and seat-shooting rules were not found. Free Fire stat scales differ between sources, so treat its km/h figures as approximate.
- Vehicle counts per match are rarely published beyond the Apex figure.

### Takeaway
Delhi is mainly a two-wheeler city (about two-thirds of registrations), and the strongest picks are the auto, the Bullet-style bike, the tractor and a moving DTC bus. Real speeds cannot be copied: a legal e-rickshaw (6.9 m/s) is slower than a Panga sprint (7.4 m/s), and a real Bullet crosses the map in 16 seconds.

### Inferences

**Why real speed fails.** E-rickshaw 6.9 m/s and tractor 8.2 m/s barely match a Panga sprint, while Bullet 30.6 m/s and Maruti 800 40 m/s would cross 500 m in 12 to 16 seconds. Compress everything into about 9 to 17 m/s, which is 1.2 to 2.3 times sprint.

**Proposal table for Connaught Place.** Health is checked against Tamancha at 80 per second: the bike lasts about 2 s of full fire, the auto about 4 s, the tractor about 12 s.

**Picks.**
1. Keep for v1: auto, Bullet-style bike, tractor (rare), DTC bus on rails. They give four clear roles and four clear Delhi images.
2. Next: Gypsy or SUV, scooter, e-rickshaw for its silence.
3. Legendary idea: a rare phat-phat as the loudest thing on the map. It is pure old-Delhi nostalgia and far less obvious than a Bullet.
4. Skip for now: drivable bus and tanker. A 12 m bus has almost no room to turn around in a 13 m street (`crates/shared/src/map/delhi.rs`).

**Vehicles as hides.** A parked bus or tempo can double as a walk-in hide and cover, since parked props already work as cover.

**Delhi jokes that fit.** "Thar aur Bullet" status, pataka silencers, tanker mafia, and a white sarkari car with a red beacon as a rare satirical find. Keep them generic: no real officer's name and no police emblem.

## What is the culture of the tractor in the Delhi region, and what is sensitive?

### Takeaway
The tractor means two things at once: rural pride and joy (tochan contests, wedding stunts, Punjabi music), and the living symbol of the 2020 to 2021 farmers' movement, with real deaths attached to both. Use the mela side and keep it away from police, barricades, Red Fort, flags and Republic Day imagery.

### Cited Findings

**Joy and pride**
- Tractor tochan is an annual head-to-head tournament that has become highly competitive in Punjab and Haryana — [VICE](https://vice.com/en/article/tractor-stunts-gone-wrong-are-killing-people-in-india)
- It works like tug of war, with two tractors pulling at opposite ends of a chain until one is dragged over a central line — [search summary of Tribune coverage](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/punjab-imposes-ban-on-tractor-stunts-as-man-dies-at-event-557944)
- One stuntman has competed in at least 150 tournaments with prizes up to Rs 15,000, and stunt riders earn up to Rs 50,000 for pre-wedding shows — [VICE](https://vice.com/en/article/tractor-stunts-gone-wrong-are-killing-people-in-india)
- A normal tractor cannot do stunts; it needs special tyres, weight and engine — [VICE](https://vice.com/en/article/tractor-stunts-gone-wrong-are-killing-people-in-india)

**Music and grief**
- Sidhu Moose Wala's body was carried on a trailer pulled by his HMT 5911 tractor, which had featured in many of his videos — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/sidhu-moosewala-makes-final-journey-on-favourite-tractor-thousands-join-procession-1114090.html)
- The 5911 grew more popular in Punjab after his death, and at his last rites one fan showed a tattoo of him with the number beneath — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/gurdaspur-farmer-buys-sidhu-moosewalas-favourite-hmt-5911-tractor-comes-to-meet-late-singers-father-476236); [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/news/india/fans-throng-punjab-village-moosa-sidhu-moose-wala-last-rites-bid-goodbye)

**Stunt deaths**
- At least seven people died in tractor stunts across India in 2020, as of October — [VICE](https://vice.com/en/article/tractor-stunts-gone-wrong-are-killing-people-in-india)
- Sukhmandeep Singh, 29, died after being run over at a fair in Gurdaspur district on 28 October 2023 — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/punjab-bans-tractor-stunts-after-29-year-old-dies-in-gurdaspur-20648)
- Punjab's CM announced a ban on 30 October 2023, writing "Do not make it a messenger of death" — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/punjab-bans-tractor-stunts-after-29-year-old-dies-in-gurdaspur-20648)
- Nishu Deswal, 22, known as "Tochan King" with about 1.3 million YouTube followers, died on 26 February 2024 when a tractor raised on its rear wheels overturned — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/star-youtube-stuntman-dies-during-tractor-act-594994/amp)

**The farmers' movement**
- The protest ran from 9 August 2020 to 11 December 2021, mainly by farmers from Punjab and Haryana, with the most widespread protests in those states and western UP — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%932021_Indian_farmers%27_protest)
- Parliament repealed the farm laws on 29 November 2021 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%932021_Indian_farmers%27_protest)
- Farmer deaths are disputed: 248 confirmed by 5 March 2021, over 537 per Samyukt Kisan Morcha, about 750 per Rakesh Tikait, 789 in the infobox — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%932021_Indian_farmers%27_protest)
- On 26 January 2021, tractor convoys entered Delhi and some left approved routes. Police estimated about 7,000 tractors at Singhu, while Reuters citing unions reported close to 200,000 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Farmers%27_Republic_Day_protest)

- In the February 2024 Delhi Chalo march, the Punjab and Haryana High Court said orally that tractor-trolleys cannot run on highways under the Motor Vehicles Act — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/tractor-trolleys-cant-run-on-highways-farmers-can-go-to-delhi-by-bus-says-punjab-and-haryana-high-court-592796)

### Inferences

**Safe to use**
- The tractor as a big, slow, loud, joyful machine.
- Tochan as a fair-style event or a win pose.
- Mud, music, bass and pride.
- A Haryanvi or Punjabi-feeling driver voice, kept affectionate and not mocking.

**Avoid**
- Tractors ramming police, barricades or DTC buses. All three echo real events on 26 January 2021, and a moving bus plus a tractor could recreate that image by accident, so test for it.
- Tractors at Red Fort, or at India Gate and Kartavya Path. `MapId::IndiaGate` exists, and a tractor there on a national-day theme would read as politics. Keep tractors off that map.
- Any religious or union flag on a tractor, and any Republic Day event.
- Names like "Kisan Andolan", "Delhi Chalo", "Tochan King" or "5911", any real person's name or likeness, and any Moose Wala song. The grief is recent and the music is copyrighted.
- A scored wheelie stunt. The nose can lift as a handling quirk, but rewarding the stunt that killed real young men is a needless risk. Punjab's ban makes this worse.

**Where the tractor belongs.** It looks out of place in CP, which can be a good joke, but its natural home is the rural edge of the final NCR map. On CP, treat it as a rare late surprise. Avoid implying "farmers invading Delhi", for instance by giving it no convoy and no mass spawn.

**Trolley.** Start with the tractor alone. A trolley makes a 7 to 10 m rig (3.6 m tractor plus 3 to 6 m trolley) with a hinge, which is far harder than one rigid box.

### Gaps
- No example found of a game drawing a complaint in India over tractor or protest imagery, so the "avoid" list is judgement, not precedent. An Indian playtester from Punjab or Haryana should review it.
- Caste pride in tractor songs (Jutt identity) is likely sensitive too, but I did not read the Caravan piece on it, so it is not covered here: [Guns, Jutts, Glory](https://caravanmagazine.in/culture/hollow-cult-sidhu-moose-wala-punjab-rap).
- Song-level facts such as "Tochan" lyrics or meaning were not found, and the search did not confirm a song by that name.

## Vehicles on a small 500 m map: what do small-map battle royales do, and are vehicles worth it?

### Takeaway
PUBG's two smallest PC maps (Haven and Karakin) give players no drivable vehicles at all, and Connaught Place is smaller and denser than both. On CP vehicles are not needed for travel, so they are only worth building as moments and identity, in small numbers, with the real travel value arriving on the 2.2 km NCR map.

### Cited Findings

**Other games**

| Map | Size | Players | Density per km² | Player vehicles | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PUBG Haven | 1 × 1 km | 32 | 32 | None; only AI Pillar helicopter and AI Pillar Tactical roam | [PUBG wiki](https://pubg.wiki.gg/wiki/Haven) |
| PUBG Karakin | 2 × 2 km | 64 | 16 | Wiki lists "N/A" | [PUBG wiki](https://pubg.wiki.gg/wiki/Karakin) |
| PUBG Mobile Livik | 2 × 2 km | 52 | 13 | Yes, including a monster truck; about 15 minute matches | [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/gamers-live/2020-07-07-pubg-mobile-gets-15-minute-livik-map/); [Slashgear](https://www.slashgear.com/pubg-mobile-adds-livik-map-made-specifically-for-phones-and-tablets-05627674) |
| PUBG Sanhok | 4 × 4 km | not stated | n/a | Tukshai, Motorcycle, Scooter, Buggy, Rony, boats | [PUBG wiki](https://pubg.wiki.gg/wiki/Sanhok) |
| Panga CP | 0.5 × 0.5 km | 16 | 64 | Proposed here | `crates/shared/src/map/delhi.rs`, `crates/shared/src/sim/mod.rs` |

- Warzone's Rebirth Island had vehicles, and Raven disabled all of them around 11 April 2022 after players used them to glitch into Golden Vaults. Some fans asked for them to stay off for good — [GamingBible](https://gamingbible.com/news/call-of-duty-warzone-has-disabled-vehicles-and-fans-are-livid-20220412)
- Tridents appear on only two Apex maps, Olympus and Storm Point — [Apex wiki](https://apexlegends.wiki.gg/wiki/Trident)

**Panga's numbers**
- Delhi maps are 500 m across (`HALF = 250`), and the ring lasts 450 s from a 360 m radius — `crates/shared/src/map/delhi.rs`, `crates/shared/src/sim/zone.rs`
- CP has Central Park at 112 m radius, the Inner Circle road at 125 m, blocks from 136 to 168 m, and seven radial streets 13 m wide — `crates/shared/src/map/delhi.rs`
- The Vimana already carries warriors in the air at 22 m/s, and its path comes from the shared match seed — `crates/shared/src/sim/flow.rs`, `crates/shared/src/sim/mod.rs`
- NCR is 2.2 km across (`HALF = 1,100 m`) — `crates/shared/src/map/ncr.rs`
- `docs/PLAN.md` has no vehicle work planned, and it says CP is proven first and NCR is the final map — `docs/PLAN.md`
- An older, now superseded plan kept vehicles as parked cover because moving ones add ownership, attachment, collision and replication work — `docs/ncr/masterplan/MASTERPLAN.md`

### Inferences

**The time saved is small.** Calculated from the numbers above:

| Speed | Cross 500 m | Inner Circle lap (785 m) | Cross NCR (2.2 km) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprint 7.4 m/s | 68 s | 106 s | 297 s |
| 10 m/s | 50 s | 79 s | 220 s |
| 14 m/s | 36 s | 56 s | 157 s |
| 18 m/s | 28 s | 44 s | 122 s |

- Saving about 30 seconds per crossing in a 450 s ring is minor on CP. On NCR a vehicle saves 2 to 3 minutes, which is a real change.
- The ring never needs a vehicle on CP. Its edge moves at 0.4 to 3.8 m/s inward. Even with the largest possible centre shift it peaks at 6.8 m/s in phase 1 and stays under 3.4 m/s after that, always slower than a sprint. (Assumes zone scale 1 on Delhi maps.)
- CP is twice as dense as Haven and four times as dense as Karakin, so a driver is rarely out of gun range and will be shot often. Vehicles there are risky bait and that suits them.

**Verdict.** Yes, but for feeling rather than travel. The owner asked for cars and tractors because they are Delhi. Their job on CP is spectacle, moving cover, a loud risk and a finishing ram, not getting across the map.

**Proposed rules for CP**
- 2 to 3 drivable vehicles at match start, chosen from the kerb-parked props by the shared match seed.
- 1 tractor arriving around phase 3, marked on the map like Free Fire's Monster Truck airdrop, so it becomes an event.
- Top speed 9 to 17 m/s with no fuel.
- Ram damage capped around 40 to 80 so a ram finishes a fight and doesn't replace guns (warrior total is 200 with full shield). Fortnite's ATK shows that low ram damage is viable.
- Vehicles stay out of colonnades and shop interiors, which is the Rebirth Island lesson.

**Cheapest first step.** Copy Haven's idea of vehicles as map hazards: run one DTC bus around the Inner Circle on a fixed path computed from the match clock. Every peer gets the same answer with no network messages, the same way the Vimana's seeded path works. It adds Delhi feeling and tests moving collision and bot avoidance before any driving code exists.

**Build order, one proven step at a time** (the owner's working rule):
1. Bus on rails.
2. Auto with a driver only, single player against bots.
3. Auto online.
4. Bike.
5. Tractor.
6. Passengers, only if teams arrive.

### Gaps
- No published data on how often players use vehicles on small maps, or on whether vehicles raise or lower retention.
- A snippet mentioned bicycles on Karakin, and I could not trace it. The wiki says "N/A".
- Current vehicle status on Rebirth Island and Fortune's Keep is unknown, and their sizes were not found.
- Whether Panga's zone uses scale 1 on Delhi maps was assumed from the code comments, not traced end to end.

## Can real names like Mahindra, Bajaj or Maruti be used, or are made-up brands safer?

### Takeaway
Use made-up brands and original shapes. Big games either pay for real brands or invent their own, and Indian law gives companies with a reputation in India a strong claim even for unrelated goods, while a commercial game has weak parody cover.

### Cited Findings
- BGMI's Mahindra BE 6 tie-up in January 2025 was its first with an Indian vehicle brand, and it included a chance to win a real car — [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/mktg/mahindra-bgmi-krafton-be6-8635464); [Esports.net](https://www.esports.net/news/mobile-games/bgmi-mahindra-be6-car-skin-interview/)
- Earlier BGMI car collaborations include McLaren, Tesla, Koenigsegg, Bugatti and Lamborghini — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/bgmi/news-bgmi-x-lamborghini-collaboration-bring-exclusive-vehicle-skins-events); [India TV](https://www.indiatvnews.com/technology/gaming/krafton-and-bugatti-collaborate-to-add-new-luxury-cars-in-bgmi-know-more-2023-06-21-877104)
- GTA's Grotti, Declasse, Vapid and Pegassi are invented stand-ins for real makers — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/gta/5-famous-vehicle-manufacturers-gta-series-real-life-inspirations)
- Giant Bomb says games invent car brands because licences are hard and costly, and argues games with car damage tend to invent theirs. It also notes little evidence of car-brand lawsuits — [Giant Bomb](https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Concepts/Fictional_Car_Brands)
- US: AM General lost its Humvee trademark case against Activision in 2020 under the Rogers test, because realism was an artistic goal and the use did not explicitly mislead — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/judge-rules-that-call-of-duty-can-have-humvees-in-it-because-games-are-art); [Finnegan](https://www.finnegan.com/en/insights/blogs/incontestable/in-legal-warfare-over-humvee-trademarks-the-first-amendment-goes-beyond-the-call-of-duty-in-dismissing-am-generals-claims.html)
- India, statute: section 29(4) of the Trade Marks Act covers use on dissimilar goods where the mark has a reputation in India and the use, without due cause, takes unfair advantage or causes harm — [IIPRD](https://www.iiprd.com/?p=12884)
- A 2025 Delhi Division Bench said "reputation" here is a lower bar than "well-known mark", and a prima facie showing is enough at the interim stage — [Cyril Amarchand](https://corporate.cyrilamarchandblogs.com/2025/05/weather-crompton-pebble-and-pebble-are-similar-or-identical/)
- India, game precedent: in Tata Sons v Greenpeace, the Delhi High Court refused an injunction against the campaign game "Turtle vs Tata", which used the Tata mark. Tata had sought Rs 10 crore — [Columbia Global Freedom of Expression](https://globalfreedomofexpression.columbia.edu/cases/tata-sons-ltd-v-greenpeace-international/); [Mallesons](https://pulse.mallesons.com/ip-whiteboard/turtle-vs-tata-pacs-a-punch/)
- One commentary reads that use as parodic and not commercial, and notes Indian trademark law has no express parody defence — [Khurana & Khurana](https://www.khuranaandkhurana.com/2013/02/27/trade-mark-parody-an-analysis-of-tata-sons-v-greenpeace/); [Khurana & Khurana 2025](https://www.khuranaandkhurana.com/2025/05/13/the-fine-line-between-parody-and-trademark-infringement-legal-battles-in-india/)
- Mahindra enforces actively: a March 2026 Delhi HC permanent injunction protected MAHINDRA against packers and movers — [SupremeToday](https://supremetoday.ai/delhi-hc-functus-officio-bars-post-decree-dynamic-ip-injunctions-20260319008)
- Vehicle shape can be protected too: the US ITC held that Mahindra's Roxor violated Fiat Chrysler's Jeep trade dress — [The IP Press](https://www.theippress.com/2020/08/22/mahindras-roxor-versus-fiat-chryslers-jeep-us-trade-dress-infringement-case)
- The Ambassador name was sold to Peugeot in 2017 for Rs 80 crore — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindustan_Ambassador)

### Inferences
- The AM General win is a US case. Panga's first audience and its likely legal risk are both in India, so it gives limited comfort.
- Tata v Greenpeace helps less than it looks: that was a non-commercial campaign with a protest message, while Panga would be a commercial product using the brand for appeal.
- The risk is highest where the game damages the brand: Mahindra-badged vehicles exploding or running people over, and any tractor badge linked to protest images.
- Recommended practice:
  - Use plain Hindi and Delhi words for vehicle types: Auto, Tempo, Phat-Phat, Tractor, Gaadi.
  - Invent a small fake motor world in the GTA style, with fun badges and truck-art lettering. The repo's made-up shop signs already follow this approach.
  - Change grilles, badges and body lines so no model is a copy. Avoid Scorpio or Thar grille lookalikes.
  - Avoid exact model numbers (575, 855, 5911, RE, 800).
  - Treat a real brand tie-up as a marketing deal to chase after launch, as BGMI did, not a build requirement. Mahindra has already done one.
- Government bodies deserve the same care: use a fictional bus operator and generic police-style livery instead of DTC or Delhi Police marks.

### Gaps
- No Indian case found on a car or bike brand appearing in a commercial video game.
- Later US treatment of the AM General ruling and the Rogers test was not checked.
- Everyday words may still be marks, including Jeep, Bullet, Gypsy, Scooty and Thar. A clearance search is needed, along with rules on using government emblems. This needs an Indian IP lawyer; these notes are not legal advice.
- Licence cost figures were not found.

## What physics and networking problems do small teams face with vehicles?

### Takeaway
The hard part is not engine physics but the edges: ownership handover, collisions between peers, seats, bots and map glitches. Panga's peer-to-peer owner model suits a kinematic arcade car where the driver's machine owns the vehicle, so copying its existing patterns is much cheaper than a physics-simulated car.

### Cited Findings

**How Panga works today**
- Every peer runs the whole sim, each warrior has one owner, only the owner moves it, and others follow its broadcast state, sent every tick at 60 Hz — `crates/shared/src/sim/net.rs`, `crates/shared/src/sim/mod.rs`
- Hits are decided by the attacker's peer and applied by the target's owner — `crates/shared/src/sim/net.rs`
- Character movement is kinematic — `crates/shared/src/sim/movement.rs`
- When two peers take the same pickup, the earlier take wins, by match time and then warrior id — `crates/shared/src/sim/loot.rs`
- A silent peer's bots are taken over by an heir through `TakeOver` — `crates/shared/src/sim/net.rs`
- Bots use A* on a half-metre grid sampled once per map, so moving objects are not in it — `crates/shared/src/sim/nav.rs`
- By default 15 of 16 warriors are bots — `crates/shared/src/sim/mod.rs`
- `DamageCause` has only Weapon, Pralaya and Fall, and `MoveMode` has Vimana, Freefall, Gliding and Ground — `crates/shared/src/sim/mod.rs`
- Game gravity is 22 m/s², more than double real gravity — `crates/shared/src/sim/movement.rs`

**Engine support**
- Avian's main docs mention no vehicles or wheels and say it has no built-in character controller, only `MoveAndSlide` utilities. Its `enhanced-determinism` flag is off by default — [Avian docs](https://docs.rs/avian3d/latest/avian3d/)
- Rapier has a ray-cast vehicle: one chassis body with wheels as ray-casts on spring suspension, ported from Bullet's `btRaycastVehicle` — [Rapier docs](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/bevy_plugin/vehicle_controller)
- Rapier is deterministic on one machine by default, while across computers it needs `enhanced-determinism` and Bevy's libm feature — [Rapier docs](https://www.rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/bevy_plugin/determinism)

**Lessons from others**
- Valheim simulates each object on the one owning machine, gives ownership to whoever was in range first, and never rebalances. For ships, steering was batched to the owner every 0.2 s, so "the person steering often waits a quarter second for every turn". Ownership moved only when the owner was off the ship, and then to an arbitrary passenger. This comes from a mod author's notes, not the developers — [SpreadTheLoad changelog](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/DeathMonger/SpreadTheLoad/changelog)
- Facepunch cut Rust's vehicle input lag by up to 100 ms by sending new positions immediately after the server's physics step — [Rust devblog](https://rust.facepunch.com/blog/vehicles-update)
- Glenn Fiedler's state sync runs 60 Hz on both sides. Velocities are omitted at rest, and a priority accumulator picks what to send. A jitter buffer of 4 to 5 frames absorbs clumped packets — [Gaffer On Games](https://gafferongames.com/post/state_synchronization/)
- The renderer hides corrections with an error offset that decays by factor 0.95 for errors up to 25 cm and 0.85 at 1 m or more. 256 kbit/s matched the quality of 18 Mbit/s snapshot interpolation — [Gaffer On Games](https://gafferongames.com/post/state_synchronization/)
- His VR sample uses per-object authority and ownership sequence numbers, where ownership beats authority and a held object rides inside the avatar's state as an id plus relative pose. It still uses a host as arbiter — [Gaffer On Games](https://gafferongames.com/post/networked_physics_in_virtual_reality/)
- Rocket League's GDC talk covers its networked physics — [GDC Vault](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1024972/)
- A blog says Rocket League runs physics at 120 Hz and network at 60 Hz with client prediction; this is unverified — [hone.gg](https://hone.gg/blog/fix-lag-in-rocket-league/)

**The bug bill**
- Apex's Trident patch history lists a server-crash bug that forced Sheila off the vehicle, drones stuck inside it on ramps, and a Bocek Bow scope flicker while riding — [Apex wiki](https://apexlegends.wiki.gg/wiki/Trident)
- The same history lists players from different teams riding together, players stuck in the collision box, and Heat Shields sticking to it — [Apex wiki](https://apexlegends.wiki.gg/wiki/Trident)
- Fortnite pulled the Shopping Cart and ATK over a bug — [GameRevolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/?p=414079)
- Warzone's Rebirth vehicles were disabled over a vault glitch — [GamingBible](https://gamingbible.com/news/call-of-duty-warzone-has-disabled-vehicles-and-fans-are-livid-20220412)
- PUBG delayed the Tukshai to tune its handling — [PUBG wiki](https://pubg.wiki.gg/wiki/Tukshai)

### Inferences

**Use a kinematic arcade car.**
- Move a box with a steering model (speed, yaw, grip, drift), keep wheels and body lean as visuals only, and cast rays down for kerbs and ground.
- This matches how warriors already move and needs no third-party physics.
- Three wheels stop being a physics problem, because the tilt is drawn, not simulated.
- A Rapier-style ray-cast chassis is the upgrade path if arcade feels too fake, but it needs a port to Avian.

**No lockstep.** Panga broadcasts state, so cross-platform determinism is not needed. Leave `enhanced-determinism` off for this.

**Specific problems and fixes**

| Problem | Why it bites Panga | Proposed fix |
|---|---|---|
| Who owns the vehicle | Valheim's 0.2 s batching shows the cost of steering someone else's object | The driver's peer owns it from the moment they enter, so the driver feels zero input lag |
| Two people grab one vehicle | No host to arbitrate, unlike Gaffer's sample | Reuse the `Claims` rule: earlier match time wins, then warrior id |
| Remote car position error | At 100 ms lag, a 16 m/s car is 1.6 m off, more than its 1.4 m width. A sprinting warrior is only 0.74 m off | Send velocity, extrapolate on the receiver, and use Gaffer-style visual error smoothing |
| Seated warrior jitter | Warrior and vehicle states arrive separately | Send vehicle id and seat instead of world position, as Gaffer's held object does; add a `Driving` move mode |
| Rams between two owners | Each peer sees the other late | Treat a ram like a shot: the driver's peer decides and sends `Hit`, plus a new damage cause |
| Driver leaves or disconnects | Vehicle could freeze mid-road | Extend the `TakeOver` heir logic, and have vehicles coast to a stop |
| Bots | 15 of 16 warriors are bots, and the walk grid ignores moving objects | Bots do not drive in v1, but they must dodge vehicles and shoot them; add a local avoidance check |
| Map glitches | Rebirth's vault glitch | Keep vehicles to a road-and-park mask, and safe-test every exit point so nobody lands inside a wall |
| Exit at speed | PUBG warns that exiting a moving vehicle can kill | Apply damage scaled by speed and reuse fall damage |
| Hidden work | Apex's list shows every item and ability touching vehicles | Budget time for camera, audio, sit animation, HUD and weapon rules, not only driving |

**Network cost is small.** Parked vehicles send nothing and a driven one adds a single state stream, so 2 to 3 vehicles barely matter next to 16 warriors at 60 Hz.

**Biggest risk.** Fun, not code. PUBG needed extra tuning on its auto rickshaw, so the first vehicle should be proven enjoyable in single player before any network work.

### Gaps
- Rocket League's real tick rates and methods come from an unverified blog. The GDC talk itself was not retrievable.
- Evidence on bots driving is conflicting and low quality ([VCGamers says PUBG Mobile bots never drive](https://www.vcgamers.com/news/en/peel-of-the-bottom-ai-pubg); [CyberPost says PUBG bots do, badly](https://cyberpost.co/how-do-you-spot-a-bot-in-pubg/)). Nothing was found for Fortnite.
- No reliable figures found for how long a vehicle system takes a small team to build.
- I checked `sim/net.rs` for remote smoothing and found none named there, but did not check the render side, so the existing interpolation for warriors is unknown.
- Whether Avian 0.8 has wheel or vehicle support outside its main docs page was not confirmed.
- Rapier's vehicle controller was not confirmed as cross-platform deterministic, though Panga does not need that.
