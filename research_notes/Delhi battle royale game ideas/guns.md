# Sniper rifles, gun roles and Indian street weapons for Panga

Research notes, 1 November 2026. Web sources marked (snippet) were read only as search excerpts, because the page was blocked or not opened. Unmarked web sources were opened and read. Repo facts cite a file and line. Game numbers belong to the patch shown and live values may differ. This covers game design, names, culture and law only, with nothing on making or modifying real weapons. The legal parts are research, not legal advice.

## 1. How do PUBG, Warzone, Fortnite, Apex and Free Fire balance snipers: damage, headshots, bullet drop, scope sway, ammo, rarity and glint?

### Takeaway
The top rifle in each game stays strong by being hard to get and short on ammo, not by being weak: AWM, Kraber and Free Fire's AWM all come from drops, and Kraber has only 12 shots. Warzone shows the failure case, with years of swings between one-shot and no-one-shot rules, and small-map ranked play removing one-shot downs altogether.

### Cited Findings

#### PUBG
- AWM is air-drop only, uses .300 ammo, holds 5 rounds (7 extended) and travels at 945 m/s (patch 22.2.8.1). — [PUBG Wiki: AWM](https://pubg.wiki.gg/wiki/AWM)
- AWM body damage falls from 136.5 unarmoured to 61.4 at level 3. Head damage falls from 262.5 to 118.1, so it still one-shots through the best helmet against 100 HP. — [PUBG Wiki: AWM](https://pubg.wiki.gg/wiki/AWM)
- Kar98k does about 197.5 head damage unarmoured and 88.8 on a level 3 helmet (PUBG Mobile, older patch). — [BlueStacks](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/pubg-mobile/pm-snipers-guide-en.html) (snippet)
- Kar98k, M24 and AWM one-shot up to a level 2 helmet, and only AWM one-shots level 3. — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/pubg-mobile-headshot-damages-for-different-guns) (snippet). BlueStacks gives the M24 the AWM's exact figures, which looks like an error.

#### Warzone
- In the July 2022 patch, Raven raised sniper max-damage ranges. Fast rifles reached 45 to 61 m (SP-R 45, Pelington 58, LW3 Tundra 61). Heavy rifles reached 117 to 137 m (HDR 117, ZRG 137). — [CharlieIntel](https://www.charlieintel.com/call-of-duty-warzone/warzone-pacific-july-28-patch-notes-189127/)
- Raven's stated aim in that patch was to keep aggressive snipers suited to smaller maps and give each weapon a map identity, with targeted changes if fast-aiming snipers dominated again. — [CharlieIntel](https://www.charlieintel.com/call-of-duty-warzone/warzone-pacific-july-28-patch-notes-189127/)
- The SP-R could break armour with a chest shot and kill with a headshot, so its flinch was raised and its aim speed lowered. — [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/cod-modern-warfare-warzone-patch-nerfs-overpowered-sp-r-sniper/1100-6483082/) (snippet)
- One patch raised a sniper's target flinch from 0.8 to 1.7. — [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/warzone-march-22-patch-notes) (snippet)
- Scope glint is a light reflection that shows a sniper at distance, normally from 4x magnification. When a 2021 update removed it from some scopes, players called it unfair because they could be killed before they knew the direction. — [MeinMMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/scopes-of-the-best-sniper-weapons-in-cod-warzone-are-broken-and-really-unfair,666389)
- In Resurgence Ranked Play (the small-map mode), snipers do not down in one shot, with damage capped at 299. — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/call-of-duty-game/all-rules-regulations-warzone-ranked-resurgence) (snippet)
- One-shot snipers were earlier banned from Warzone Ranked after pro backlash. — [GG Recon](https://ggrecon.com/articles/warzone-bans-one-shot-snipers-after-community-backlash) (snippet)
- The swing went both ways: a February 2023 patch removed one-shot potential, then [only three rifles kept it](https://www.wepc.com/gaming/warzone-2-sniper-rifles/) after Season 3, and later [players begged for the one-shot meta back](https://charlieintel.com/call-of-duty/warzone-players-beg-for-sniper-buffs-to-bring-back-one-shot-meta-292141). — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/warzone-2-february-24-patch-removes-one-shot-potential-snipers-season-2) (all three headline snippets)

#### Fortnite
- Heavy Sniper in v20.10 had a magazine of 1. Rare did 120 damage, 180 head, 4 s reload. Epic did 128, 192, 3.8 s. Legendary did 132, 198, 3.5 s. It also did 640+ damage to vehicles. — [GINX](https://www.ginx.tv/en/fortnite/how-to-get-fortnite-heavy-sniper-rifle-and-stats) (snippet)
- Earlier versions hit harder, at 150 to 157 damage with reloads of about 4.1 to 4.3 s. — [GINX](https://www.ginx.tv/en/fortnite/how-to-get-fortnite-heavy-sniper-rifle-and-stats) (snippet)
- Fortnite snipers are projectiles with travel time and drop, while hitscan weapons have neither. Guides disagree on when other guns moved to projectiles. — [Dexerto](https://dexerto.com/fortnite/hitscan-in-fortnite-explained-how-it-works-and-the-best-hitscan-weapons-2902782) (snippet)

#### Apex Legends
- Kraber: 150 body, 210 head, 120 legs. It holds 4 rounds, has 12 total shots and takes no outside ammo. It is found only in care packages or Mythic bins, has a fixed 6x to 10x scope and a 749 m/s projectile. — [Apex Wiki: Kraber](https://apexlegends.wiki.gg/wiki/Kraber_.50-Cal_Sniper)
- Kraber's history shows the tuning swings. Head multiplier went from x2.05 to x3 (April 2020), back to x2 (May 2022), and head damage is now listed at 210 after a February 2025 body buff to 150. — [Apex Wiki: Kraber](https://apexlegends.wiki.gg/wiki/Kraber_.50-Cal_Sniper)
- Longbow's head multiplier is listed at 2.15, or 2.5 with Skullpiercer. — [Apex Wiki](https://apexlegends.wiki.gg/wiki/Skullpiercer_Rifling) (snippet); [Liquipedia](https://liquipedia.net/apexlegends/Longbow) (snippet) says Skullpiercer support was removed.
- Sentinel does 70 body, or 88 when charged by spending a shield cell. — [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/?p=98592) (snippet)

#### Free Fire
- AWM and Kar98k have about 90 base damage and AWM has limited ammo. Kar98k cannot one-shot a level 3 helmet and is the pick when the airdrop is too risky. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/awm-vs-kar98k-which-best-sniper-rifle-free-fire) (snippet)
- M82B's selling point is armour penetration and shooting through Gloo Walls, the game's deployable cover. — [Free Fire Nation](https://freefirenation.com/free-fire-m82b-gun/) (snippet); [Free Fire Mania](https://www.freefiremania.com.br/weapon/m82b.html) (snippet). Sources conflict on its exact penetration (54 vs 67) and magazine (5 vs 8).

### Inferences
- The common pattern is that the best rifle one-shots a head through top armour but costs a dangerous trip to a drop, while the normal rifle stops one step short of that.
- Games limit snipers in two ways: the very best rifle stays out of world loot, and normal bolt rifles pay for their damage with slow reloads, such as Fortnite's 3.5 to 4 s.
- Fortnite is the closest numeric match. From general knowledge (not checked here), it also uses 100 health plus 100 shield, and its best listed sniper head hit of 198 falls just short of 200. That looks deliberate and suits Panga.
- Warzone's 299 cap reads as a limit set one point under full health, though the source does not say so.
- Bullet speed is a tuning tool, not realism. Heavy snipers everywhere stay projectiles so that long shots take skill.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable figures for scope sway in any of the five games.
- I could not confirm glint rules for PUBG, Fortnite, Apex or Free Fire. A search returned only Battlefield forum posts.
- Reserve ammo totals for Free Fire snipers were not found.
- The official Call of Duty patch page timed out, so the ranked rule rests on third-party coverage, and I could not confirm it still applies in November 2026.
- Fortnite bullet-drop evidence comes from low-quality guides, and the Fortnite wiki page was blocked (HTTP 402).

## 2. How should snipers work on a small urban map with 50 m to 200 m sight lines?

### Takeaway
Treat snipers as a limited mid-game tool, with slower bullets, 2x to 4x scopes, glint, scarce ammo and rooftops that expose the shooter. Two repo problems come first: a charged Gulel already out-damages any sensible sniper, and bots cannot target anyone beyond 70 m, so a rifle would make them free kills.

### Cited Findings
- PUBG's smallest map, Haven, is 1 x 1 km for 32 players. — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/playerunknowns-battlegrounds/season-10-haven) (snippet)
- Krafton built Haven around verticality and fights across streets and rooftops. — [VGC](https://videogameschronicle.com/news/pubg-season-10-adds-a-new-urban-map-with-a-focus-on-verticality-and-ai-combatants) (snippet)
- One guide describes Haven as built for mid-range and close-quarters fights. — [Gamertag Mythras](https://gamertagmythras.com/blog/pubg-battlegrounds/pubg-haven-map-guide) (snippet)
- PUBG's creative director said the PUBG formula works better on larger maps. — [MeinMMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/creative-director-of-pubg-in-interview-the-pubg-formula-works-better-on-larger-maps,639166) (headline snippet)
- Panga players have 100 HP and up to 100 shield, and shield absorbs damage first. — `crates/shared/src/sim/mod.rs:123-128`, `crates/shared/src/sim/combat.rs:595-597`
- Rarity multiplies damage: Common 1.0, Rare 1.08, Epic 1.16, Legendary 1.24, Divya 1.35. — `crates/shared/src/items.rs:21-28`
- A fully charged Gulel does 95 x rarity with a x2 headshot and drops only at Rare or above. That is 102.6 body and 205.2 head at Rare, up to 117.8 and 235.6 at Legendary. — `assets/data/items.ron` (gulel), `crates/shared/src/sim/combat.rs:247-250`
- Bots engage at 45 m with a ranged weapon (26 m in "chill" mode), and when shot they only answer inside 70 m. — `crates/shared/src/sim/bots.rs:98`, `crates/shared/src/sim/bots.rs:359`, `crates/shared/src/sim/bots.rs:444-445`
- A text search of `bots.rs` found no gunshot hearing. — `crates/shared/src/sim/bots.rs`
- Aiming narrows the camera from 70 to 48 degrees for every weapon, and aiming only multiplies spread by 0.6. There is no scope setting on items. — `crates/game/src/camera.rs:148-149`, `crates/shared/src/sim/combat.rs:237-238`
- Bullets follow simple gravity with a 4 s lifetime, and players walk at 5.6 m/s and sprint at 7.4 m/s. — `crates/shared/src/sim/combat.rs:251`, `crates/shared/src/sim/combat.rs:351`, `crates/shared/src/sim/movement.rs:10-11`
- All guns share one Goli reserve, sold in packs of 30. — `crates/shared/src/items.rs:69-70`, `assets/data/items.ron` (arrows)

### Inferences

#### Scale
- Panga's 0.25 sq km map is a quarter of Haven's area. With 15 fighters that is about 60 per sq km, against Haven's 32. A 200 m shot covers 40 percent of the map's width.
- The Aandhi shrinks sight lines on its own, so rifles should matter from about minute 2 to minute 6 of a 7 to 9 minute match. Late fights need a second gun.

#### Bullet feel (computed from the game's gravity code)

| Weapon | Speed m/s | Gravity | Flight to 200 m | Drop at 200 m | Lead on a sprinter at 200 m |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Vajra-9 today | 120 | 2 | 1.67 s | 2.8 m | 12.3 m |
| Katta today | 140 | 3 | 1.43 s | 3.1 m | 10.6 m |
| Gulel today (full charge) | 170 | 2 | 1.18 s | 1.4 m | 8.7 m |
| Proposed DMR | 280 | 5 | 0.71 s | 1.3 m | 5.3 m |
| Proposed bolt rifle | 400 | 6 | 0.50 s | 0.75 m | 3.7 m |
| Proposed crate rifle | 550 | 6 | 0.36 s | 0.4 m | 2.7 m |

At real game speeds (AWM 945 m/s, Kraber 749 m/s), a 200 m shot lands in 0.21 to 0.27 s, so there is almost nothing to lead on a map this small. Slower bullets of 350 to 450 m/s keep long shots a skill.

#### Starting numbers (to test in the real game, one at a time)

| Name idea | Role | Base dmg | Head x | Mag | Scope | Drops | Head dmg by rarity |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Teen-Pandrah (.315) | Entry bolt rifle | 55 | 2.0 | 5 | 2x | Common, Rare | 110, 119 |
| Thri-Not-Thri (.303) | Main bolt rifle | 70 | 2.0 | 5 | 4x | Rare, Epic, Legendary | 151, 162, 174 |
| Thaana SLR | Marksman rifle | 34 | 1.6 | 10 | 2x | Rare, Epic | 59, 63 |
| Haathi Rifle | Crate-only rifle | 75 | 2.0 | 4, about 8 total, no refill | 6x | Divya only | 202.5 |
| Gulel (retune) | Silent charge shot | 62 | 2.0 | 1 | none | Rare and up | 134 to 154 |

- With these numbers, a normal rifle kills a head through one Lassi at Rare (151 vs a 150 pool) but never through a full 200 pool. Only the crate rifle does, matching how the AWM and Kraber are gated.
- A body shot should not kill a shielded player. The crate rifle's 101 body damage kills only someone with no shield.

#### Rules
1. **Fix the Gulel first.** Today it one-shots a full 200 pool on a head at every rarity it drops, which beats any rifle above. Retune it and give it a new job as the quiet option.
2. **Fix bots before adding rifles.** When hit from beyond 70 m, a bot should break line of sight or flank. Otherwise every bot is a free kill. Under the owner's one-step-at-a-time rule, prove this in the real game before adding the next rifle.
3. **Keep scopes low.** A per-item FOV setting would do it: 2x is about 38.6 degrees, 4x about 19.9 and 6x about 13.3, measured from the 70 degree base. Today's aim view is about 1.6x.
4. **Glint from 4x and up, never on 2x.** Warzone's bug shows the value of a clear warning. Glint should show on the target's screen at any range up to 200 m.
5. **Ration ammo.** A shared 30-pack of Goli would give a rifle 30 shots, against 12 on the Kraber. Use a separate heavy ammo or a per-shot ammo cost.
6. **Make hip fire bad.** The 0.6 spread factor is too mild. With zero base spread, a rifle is accurate unscoped. Rifles need a large spread unless fully scoped.
7. **Flinch when hit while scoped,** which is Warzone's main answer to fast snipers.
8. **Keep rooftops risky.** Give good perches at least two routes up, hoardings and trees that break long lines, and no single perch that sees the whole map. This is a level design call for the hiding-places research.
9. **Add anti-vehicle damage,** following Fortnite's 640+ figure, so a rifle is a real answer once cars and tractors arrive.

### Gaps
- I found no measured sight-line data for Panga's Connaught Place map or for Haven snipers.
- No source covers third-person peeking over cover, which matters because Panga's camera can see past corners. Treat it as a known risk to test.
- Bot aim at long range was not reviewed.

## 3. What real guns are known in India, and which feel locally recognisable?

### Takeaway
The .303 is the best sniper base because it carries real police history and nostalgia, and it has only recently left service. For Delhi police flavour the present mix is SLR, INSAS, AK-47, Glock and MP5, and country-made pistols and 12 bores are already well covered by Katta, Tamancha and Dunali.

### Cited Findings
- Delhi Police planned to dismantle at least 7,000 British-era .303 rifles, whose use ended years earlier and whose ammunition has run out (November 2023). — [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/delhi/delhi-police-to-bid-adieu-to-7-000-british-era-303-rifles-563692)
- The same report says the Army used them in the 1962 war before passing them to state forces, and that Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra had already retired theirs. — [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/delhi/delhi-police-to-bid-adieu-to-7-000-british-era-303-rifles-563692)
- Delhi Police long-range arms are SLR, INSAS and AK-47, short-range arms are 9 mm Glock pistols and revolvers, and JVPC and MP5 are used in special operations. — [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/delhi/delhi-police-to-bid-adieu-to-7-000-british-era-303-rifles-563692)
- Old Sten guns held in large numbers in the Delhi armoury are expected to be dismantled next. — [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/delhi/delhi-police-to-bid-adieu-to-7-000-british-era-303-rifles-563692)

## 4. What full weapon set do popular games use by role, and which roles are missing from the Panga list?

### Takeaway
Panga covers close range well but has almost nothing between 45 m and 150 m. It lacks a true assault rifle, a marksman rifle, a real sniper, a light machine gun and, for the owner's hiding goal, a smoke grenade.

- Indus, the Indian battle royale, runs assault rifles (SFR-4, A27 Locust), an SMG (V-Fury), a shotgun (Kismet CS-10), a sniper (Vantage), a hand cannon (Judgement) and a limited-ammo Yaksha special weapon (ULKA). — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/mobile-games/indus-battle-royale-weapon-tier-list) (snippet); [GamingonPhone](https://gamingonphone.com/guides/indus-battle-royale-mobile-weapon-tier-list) (snippet)

## 5. What funny or local weapon ideas fit Delhi, such as slingshots, firecrackers, cricket items or a jugaad scope?

### Takeaway
Joke items last when they have a real mechanic, as PUBG's Pan shows. Panga's best chance is to turn everyday Delhi objects into utility: Gulal for smoke, Ladi for decoy noise, a Tawa that stops bullets and joke scopes.

### Cited Findings
- PUBG's Pan is a melee weapon that also blocks bullets, including the AWM's, whether equipped or carried. It does 80 body and 120 head against no armour, and the wiki calls it the best melee weapon. — [PUBG Wiki: Pan](https://pubg.wiki.gg/wiki/Pan)
- PUBG's other melee weapons are everyday tools: Crowbar, Machete and Sickle. — [PUBG Wiki: Pan](https://pubg.wiki.gg/wiki/Pan)
- Panga's Katta already has a 15 percent misfire and 3 percent backfire chance. — `assets/data/items.ron` (katta)
- Apex's Sentinel spends a consumable to power up a gun. — [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/?p=98592) (snippet)
- After Haryana's crackdown, lathis are replacing guns in Haryanvi music videos. — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/feature/lathis-replacing-guns-in-haryana-music-videos-crackdown-is-bringing-tamer-lyrics-now/2632850) (headline snippet)

### Inferences

All of these are original ideas, sorted by how much they add to play.

#### Throwables and utility
- **Gulal Bomb** (Holi colour): the smoke grenade Panga lacks, in bright pink or green. This is the top pick because it directly serves "places to hide".
- **Ladi** (firecracker string): rattles like gunfire to pull players away. Bots would need to hear gunfire first.
- **Pichkari** (Holi water gun): marks a target in colour for a few seconds, so they cannot hide.
- **Pani Gubbara** (water balloon): washes off colour and puts out Anaar fire.
- **Mirchi Bomb** (chilli powder): a blinding cloud that replaces a flashbang.
- **Chakri** (ground spinner): moving fire patch that skids unpredictably.
- **Rocket** (bottle rocket): a flare that marks an area or calls a crate.
- **Dengue Fogger** (municipal fogging machine): a carried smoke maker that leaves a trail through a lane.

#### Cricket items
- **Tape Ball**: a bouncing grenade that can be skimmed under cars.
- **Stump Mic**: a planted item that shows footsteps nearby.
- **Third Umpire**: slang name for the kill replay rather than a weapon.

#### Melee
- **Tawa** (griddle): the Pan's idea, carried on the back to stop one bullet.

## 6. Are real gun names a legal risk in games? How do games name them?

### Takeaway
The US position favours games, but no Indian ruling was found, and the safest route is also the most local one: use Hindi nicknames and calibre words instead of brand names. Panga gains nothing from "Glock" or "MP5", and Katta, Dunali and Thri-Not-Thri are stronger for this game anyway.

### Cited Findings
- In 2013 EA stopped paying gun makers licence fees but kept depicting the guns, claiming a free-speech right. — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2013-05-08-ea-kills-licensing-deals-with-gun-makers-keeps-those-guns-in-ga.html) (snippet)
- In AM General v. Activision (S.D.N.Y., March 2020), Call of Duty won over Humvees under the Rogers test, because the use had artistic relevance and did not mislead buyers. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/judge-rules-that-call-of-duty-can-have-humvees-in-it-because-games-are-art) (snippet); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/trademark/925408/in-legal-warfare-over-humvee-trademarks-the-first-amendment-goes-beyond-the-call-of-duty-in-dismissing-am-generals-claims) (snippet)
- An Indian IP firm's article explains that case but cites no Indian law or Indian ruling, notes that some user polls showed confusion, and advises taking specific advice. — [Khurana & Khurana via Legal 500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/india/intellectual-property/creating-video-games-make-sure-not-to-infringe-other%27s-trademark)
- Remington's 2009 deal to put a rifle in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2 came to light through the Sandy Hook litigation, which settled for $73 million and did not target Activision. — [SAN](https://san.com/?p=118160) (snippet); [Inven Global](https://labs.invenglobal.com/articles/16538/call-of-duty-sandy-hook-and-the-73m-lawsuit-that-saw-the-end-of-remington) (snippet)
- Uvalde families sued Activision in 2024, and Activision argued First Amendment protection in 2025. — [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/fps/uvalde-activision-lawsuit) (snippet); [Insurance Journal](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/southcentral/2025/07/24/833015.htm) (snippet)
- An advocacy report claimed real make-and-model guns do not sell games better than made-up names. — [Mediaite](https://www.mediaite.com/online/video-game-companies-pressured-to-drop-gun-manufacturer-product-placement) (snippet); treat this as an advocacy claim.
- PUBG uses real names, such as AWM, Karabiner 98 Kurz, M24, Mosin Nagant and Winchester Model 1894. — [PUBG Wiki](https://pubg.wiki.gg/wiki/AWM)
- Free Fire uses real-sounding names (AWM, Kar98k, M82B). — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/awm-vs-kar98k-which-best-sniper-rifle-free-fire) (snippet)
- Apex uses invented names such as Longbow DMR and Sentinel. — [Apex Wiki](https://apexlegends.wiki.gg/wiki/Skullpiercer_Rifling) (snippet)
- Fortnite uses plain descriptions such as Heavy Sniper Rifle. — [GINX](https://www.ginx.tv/en/fortnite/how-to-get-fortnite-heavy-sniper-rifle-and-stats) (snippet)
- Indus, an Indian studio's game, says all its guns are made up, though some resemble real ones. — [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/indus/release-date/) (April 2023)

### Inferences
- Treat the risk by name type. Generic Hindi words (Katta, Tamancha, Dunali, Gulel, Bandook) look safest. Calibre words and old names (Thri-Not-Thri, Bara Bore, Sten, SLR) look fairly safe. Current brands (Glock, MP5, Heckler & Koch) and product names are worth avoiding.
- Indus is the best local precedent. An Indian studio chose invented names with loose real-world shapes.
- The Uvalde case shows the real long-term risk: being seen as marketing for a gun. Never link to a gun maker, take brand money or copy a real gun exactly.
- Visual design should follow the same rule. Use a recognisable silhouette with changed details, such as taped grips and jugaad repairs, which also fits the theme.
- US rulings may offer little protection for an Indian studio, so a short check with an Indian IP lawyer before launch is worthwhile.

- I found no Indian court decision on real product names in games.
- I did not check who holds rights in "AK-47", "Kalashnikov" or "INSAS" in India.
- I found no confirmed outcome for the Uvalde motion or Bell Helicopter's case against EA.
- I could not confirm whether Call of Duty moved to fictional names after 2013.

## 7. What are the content rating and legal issues in India for guns in games, including IGRS ratings and past bans?

### Takeaway
"IGRS" looks like a mix-up: in the sources found it means Indonesia's system, no Indian system by that name turned up, and India's own is the voluntary BIS standard IS 19690:2026. Past national bans came from data and China-link concerns, not guns, so Panga's near-term risks are outrage over gun glorification and religious names, plus staying clear of money-game rules.

### Cited Findings

#### Ratings
- IGRS in search results means the Indonesia Game Rating System. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia_Game_Rating_System) (snippet)
- Its Steam rollout drew heavy criticism. — [Niko Partners](https://nikopartners.com/indonesia-game-rating-system-heavily-criticized-on-its-rollout/) (snippet)
- BIS notified IS 19690:2026 on 10 March 2026, as India's first formal age-rating and content-descriptor standard. — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-originals/indias-first-game-rating-standard-real-need-or-overreach)
- The bands are U/A 0+, 3+, 7+, 13+, 16+ and A (Adults Only), with A linked to graphic violence and explicit material. — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-originals/indias-first-game-rating-standard-real-need-or-overreach)
- The standard is voluntary with no enforcement planned at the time of writing, uses self-assessment, and could become binding through a Quality Control Order or a MeitY notification. — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-originals/indias-first-game-rating-standard-real-need-or-overreach)
- Descriptors cover items such as violence, language and sexual content. — [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-bis-age-rating-standard-video-games-india/) (snippet); [Law.asia](https://law.asia/india-gaming-age-ratings/) (snippet)

#### Online gaming law
- The Online Gaming Rules 2026 were notified in April and took effect on 1 May 2026. — [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-online-gaming-rules-notified-may-1-major-changes/) (snippet); [Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=822e9f46-659b-4c32-aa76-6045645a9351) (snippet)

### Inferences
- Correct the brief: use "IS 19690:2026" for India and treat IGRS only as an Indonesian Steam question.
- Self-assess against IS 19690 anyway, because it costs little and may become binding. A stylised look without gore could plausibly sit around U/A 13+ or 16+, but this is a guess until the descriptor text is read.
- Panga should qualify as an online social game if it has no stakes. Do not run paid-entry tournaments with cash prizes, which look like the line into money games. Formal e-sports would need registration.
- National bans so far point to data and foreign-link risk, not gun content. An Indian-run game with simple data handling is on safer ground, though the Rajkot order shows local police can act on violence and exam-season worries alone.
- Gun glorification is the bigger near-term risk for Panga. Frame guns as chaotic jugaad and comedy rather than gangster aspiration. Keep away from real gangs, and keep Katta lore funny rather than boastful.
- Renaming Trishul Carbine and Vajra-9 is worth doing. The complaints found were about gods as characters, not gun names, but a gun named after a god's weapon seems an easy target, and the owner's new direction is street Delhi anyway. Removing the Bharat map has already moved the game away from mythology. The Sudarshan Chakra and Brahmastra names and lore deserve the same review.
- A short legal review before launch is cheaper than a launch-week row over any of these names.
